社区医院挂号

技能
读
时间
约 20 分钟
做完
写明代词指什么，答完三问，并写下下一个修改点

Ping 去社区医院挂号，邻居 Wen 也在大厅。这一课盯住 it、them 和 they。号条、检查单、药都会被指回去。写在这一页的框里。只留在这次浏览，不上传。

材料

这是一篇约 240 词的短文。不认识的词，最多查一个。不列词表，也不整篇翻译。

On Tuesday morning, Ping takes a number at the community clinic. Wen is already sitting near the window with a folder. They nod and wait for the screen to call names. Ping’s number lights up first. She walks to desk three and shows her ID. The nurse prints a small ticket and hands it over. Ping folds it into her pocket. Wen’s turn comes ten minutes later. The doctor asks about her cough and listens to her chest. He writes two notes and signs them. Wen carries them to the pharmacy window. Ping meets her there after a blood pressure check. They compare the waiting times on their phones. A volunteer points them to the payment machine. Ping pays for her visit and keeps the receipt. She puts it next to the ticket. Wen picks up a bottle of pills and shakes it once. The pharmacist reminds her to take them after meals. Outside they share an umbrella because it starts to rain. Ping holds it while Wen locks her bike. They walk to the bus stop together. Ping gets off two stops earlier. Wen stays on and texts that the pills are sealed. At home Ping pins the receipt to a board. It stays there until she files the clinic visit in her app. She washes her hands and drinks warm water. Later Wen asks to borrow the umbrella back. Ping returns it the next morning. The ticket in her pocket is creased but still clear. She throws it away after the report appears online.

步骤

  1. 读一遍。用中文说主要的人做了什么。
  2. 再读一遍。每个 it、them、they 写下指什么。
  3. 回答下面三问。先写下来，再打开核对。

三个问题

三问都写在下面，才能打开核对。只留在这次浏览，不上传，也不打分。

三问都写一点，再打开核对。

再来一轮

三问写完以后，把这几个词按原句的顺序点上去。大约半分钟。排完再打开下面折着的核对。

写下以后再看核对
  • folds it / throws it：号条；puts it：收据；shakes it：药瓶；holds it / returns it：雨伞；It stays：收据
  • signs them / take them：单子／药片
  • They nod / They compare / They walk：Ping 和 Wen

三问

  1. 护士打印的那张小号条／票。
  2. 医生写的两张单子。
  3. 雨伞。

给中文使用者的一处

it 在这篇里换得很快：号条、收据、药瓶、雨伞。每一处都先看前面最近的单数名词。

只留一个修改点

看完核对，只写下一处下次会改的地方。

  • 如果把 “returns it” 的 it 当成了药瓶，下次先看 borrow the umbrella。只改这一处。
  • 如果都对了，修改点就写：下次读到 them 或 it，仍然先找前面的名词，不改去抄生词。

两件都写，等于没有重点。只留一个。

这课不练什么

不练听，不练说，也不把这课收成单词本。写在「写」里，不在这课。
