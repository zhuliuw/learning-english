晚点的那趟车

技能
读
时间
约 20 分钟
做完
写明每个 it 指什么，答完三问，并写下下一个修改点

Lin Gao 和同事 Anna 从办公室走到车站，火车晚点，再坐到各自的那一站。这一课还是代词，这一次盯住 it。it 不是人。它有时是站台上的显示板，有时才是那趟火车。写在这一页的框里。只留在这次浏览，不上传。

材料

这是从办公室到家的晚点火车，约 234 词。不认识的词，最多查一个。不列词表，也不整篇翻译。

Lin Gao leaves the office at 17:50 and walks to the station with his colleague Anna. The street is busy, and they do not talk much on the way. Lin Gao waits for the train home. People stand in a loose line along the platform, and a few people check the time. The board above the platform changes. It says the train will come at 18:40, not 18:10. Someone near the bench sighs, and a woman moves her bag to a seat. His colleague Anna stands next to him. She asks whether it is their usual train. Lin Gao says yes. It stops three times before their station. Anna takes a book from her bag and reads. She looks up when the crowd moves. The delay is half an hour, so they stay on the platform and do not look for another train. The train arrives, and the doors open. Lin Gao follows Anna inside. They find two seats by the door. The ride is quiet. Anna keeps reading, and Lin Gao watches the dark window. At his stop, Lin Gao stands up. Anna stays seated because her stop is the next one. He waves and steps onto the platform. The doors close, and the train moves on without him. Lin Gao walks the last streets home and does not hurry. At home he heats yesterday's rice and does not turn on the news.

步骤

  1. 读一遍。用中文说 Lin Gao 在等什么。
  2. 再读一遍。每个 it 写下指什么。she、he、they 可以顺手标上，但问题只考 it。
  3. 回答下面三问。先写下来，再打开核对。

三个问题

三问都写在下面，才能打开核对。只留在这次浏览，不上传，也不打分。

三问都写一点，再打开核对。

写下以后再看核对
  • It says：站台上的 board，不是火车
  • whether it is their usual train：那趟火车
  • It stops three times：那趟火车
  • they do not talk / they stay / They find：Lin Gao 和 Anna
  • She asks / She looks：Anna
  • He waves / he heats：Lin Gao

三问

  1. 站台上方的显示板。
  2. 那趟火车。
  3. 那趟火车。

给中文使用者的一处

it 不是人。这一篇里它有时是站台上的显示板，有时是火车。先看 it 前面最近的那个事物，再决定。不要把每个 it 都当成那趟车。

只留一个修改点

看完核对，只写下一处下次会改的地方。

  • 如果把 “It says” 的 it 当成了火车，下次先看它前面的名词是 board 还是 train。只改这一处。
  • 如果都对了，修改点就写：下次读到 it，仍然先找它前面的事物，不改去抄生词。

两件都写，等于没有重点。只留一个。

这课不练什么

不练听，不练说，也不把这课收成单词本。阅读到这里有五课。写在「写」里，不在这课。
