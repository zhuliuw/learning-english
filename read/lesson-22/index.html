同事婚礼

技能
读
时间
约 20 分钟
做完
写明代词指什么，答完三问，并写下下一个修改点

周六同事举办婚礼，Ava 去观礼，同事 Dom 一起坐桌。这一课盯住 it、them 和 they。请柬、礼物、座位都会被指回去。写在这一页的框里。只留在这次浏览，不上传。

材料

这是一篇约 240 词的短文。不认识的词，最多查一个。不列词表，也不整篇翻译。

On Saturday afternoon, Ava pins the wedding invite to her fridge one last time. She folds it into her bag before she leaves. Dom meets her at the hotel lobby. They find their names on the seating board. Ava carries a wrapped gift and sets it on the gift table. A staff member moves the gifts and stacks them carefully. Dom takes two programs and hands one to Ava. She reads it during the short wait. The ceremony starts on time. They stand when the couple enters. Ava wipes her eyes once and laughs about it later. After the vows Dom leads them to table nine. Ava hangs her coat on the chair and smooths it. Servers bring dishes and refill them through the meal. Ava tastes the soup and nods. Dom takes photos and sends them to the team chat. Ava likes the clearest one and saves it. Near the end they sign a small guest book. Ava writes a short line and dots it. Dom adds his name under hers. Outside they wait for a taxi in the cool air. Ava holds the program so it does not blow away. Dom opens the car door and closes it after her. At home Ava hangs the invite on a board. It stays there until she files the photos. Dom messages that the gift table was full. Ava answers it before she sleeps. On Monday they tell coworkers the day went well. Ava returns the spare umbrella she borrowed. Dom puts it back by the office door.

步骤

  1. 读一遍。用中文说主要的人做了什么。
  2. 再读一遍。每个 it、them、they 写下指什么。
  3. 回答下面三问。先写下来，再打开核对。

三个问题

三问都写在下面，才能打开核对。只留在这次浏览，不上传，也不打分。

三问都写一点，再打开核对。

再来一轮

三问写完以后，把这几个词按原句的顺序点上去。大约半分钟。排完再打开下面折着的核对。

写下以后再看核对
  • folds it / sets it / reads it / about it / smooths it / saves it / dots it / so it does not / closes it / It stays / answers it / puts it：请柬、礼物、节目单、大衣、照片、句子、车门、消息、伞等
  • stacks them / refill them / sends them / leads them：礼物／菜／照片／两人入座
  • They find / They stand / they sign / they wait / they tell：Ava 和 Dom

三问

  1. 婚礼请柬。
  2. 礼桌上的礼物。
  3. 那把借来的伞。

给中文使用者的一处

最后的 it 指伞，不是请柬。看 returns the spare umbrella。

只留一个修改点

看完核对，只写下一处下次会改的地方。

  • 如果把最后的 it 当成了请柬，下次先看 returns the spare umbrella。只改这一处。
  • 如果都对了，修改点就写：下次读到 them 或 it，仍然先找前面的名词，不改去抄生词。

两件都写，等于没有重点。只留一个。

这课不练什么

不练听，不练说，也不把这课收成单词本。写在「写」里，不在这课。
