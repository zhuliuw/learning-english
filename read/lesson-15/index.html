办公室消防演练，Helen 带本组下楼，保安 Chen 在出口清点。这一课盯住 it、them 和 they。警报、名单、楼层都会被指回去。写在这一页的框里。只留在这次浏览，不上传。

材料

这是一篇约 240 词的短文。不认识的词，最多查一个。不列词表，也不整篇翻译。

At ten sharp the fire alarm starts on the fifth floor. Helen closes her laptop and picks up the team list. She folds it once and leads three coworkers to the stairwell. They keep to the right and do not use the elevator. Chen stands at the ground exit with a clipboard. He checks names and marks them. Helen hands him the list and waits. A manager counts the groups and waves them farther from the door. Helen moves her team under a tree. They watch the building while the drill continues. Chen radios the fifth floor and confirms it is clear. Helen hears it on the speaker nearby. Rain starts, so she opens a small umbrella and shares it. Two coworkers step under it. Chen calls the all-clear after twelve minutes. They walk back in a single line. Helen returns the list to the desk drawer. It stays there for the next drill. Chen posts a short note on the board. Helen reads it and tells the team the timing was fine. One coworker asks about the stair lights. Chen says they will be checked after lunch. Helen unlocks her laptop and saves a draft. She sends it to her manager before noon. The alarm stays quiet for the rest of the morning. Helen drinks water and stretches her shoulders. Chen collects the clipboards and stacks them. Helen thanks him and returns to her seat. The drill is over, but the list still sits in the drawer. She checks it once more before the afternoon meeting.

步骤

  1. 读一遍。用中文说主要的人做了什么。
  2. 再读一遍。每个 it、them、they 写下指什么。
  3. 回答下面三问。先写下来，再打开核对。

三个问题

三问都写在下面，才能打开核对。只留在这次浏览，不上传，也不打分。

三问都写一点，再打开核对。

再来一轮

三问写完以后，把这几个词按原句的顺序点上去。大约半分钟。排完再打开下面折着的核对。

写下以后再看核对
  • folds it / confirms it / shares it / It stays / reads it / sends it / checks it：名单、楼层清空确认、伞、告示、草稿等
  • marks them / waves them / stacks them / they will be checked：名字／各组／写字板／楼梯灯
  • They keep / They watch / They walk：Helen 和同事们

三问

  1. 小组名单。
  2. 那些名字。
  3. 她保存的那份草稿。

给中文使用者的一处

they 有时指人，有时指楼梯灯。看动词：walk、watch 多半是人；will be checked 多半是物。

只留一个修改点

看完核对，只写下一处下次会改的地方。

  • 如果把 “sends it” 的 it 当成了名单，下次先看 saves a draft。只改这一处。
  • 如果都对了，修改点就写：下次读到 them 或 it，仍然先找前面的名词，不改去抄生词。

两件都写，等于没有重点。只留一个。

这课不练什么

不练听，不练说，也不把这课收成单词本。写在「写」里，不在这课。
