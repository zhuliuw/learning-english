图书馆还书

技能
读
时间
约 20 分钟
做完
写明代词指什么，答完三问，并写下下一个修改点

Qing 去图书馆还书，遇到同学 Fang。这一课盯住 it、them 和 they。书、收据、座位都会被指回去。写在这一页的框里。只留在这次浏览，不上传。

材料

这是还书的一小时，约 240 词。不认识的词，最多查一个。不列词表，也不整篇翻译。

On Saturday morning, Qing carries four books to the city library. Fang meets her at the bike rack outside. They walk in together and join a short line. Qing sets the books on the return desk. The librarian scans them one by one. One book is a day late. Qing pays a small fine and keeps the receipt. She folds it and slides it into her notebook. Fang returns two novels of her own. The librarian stamps both cards. Qing asks where the quiet study room is. The librarian points down the hall. They find two free seats by the window. Qing opens a work email on her phone and answers it. Fang reads a magazine from the rack. After twenty minutes Qing stands to stretch. She leaves her bag on the chair. Fang watches it while Qing fills a water bottle. When Qing returns, they decide to look for a history shelf. Qing pulls out a thin book and checks the first page. She puts it back because the print is too small. Fang finds a thicker book and borrows it. At the desk Qing waits while Fang signs the form. They leave before noon. Outside Qing unlocks her bike. Fang walks toward the bus stop. Qing texts her brother that the late book is returned. She puts the phone away and rides home. At home she pins the receipt to the fridge. It stays there until she checks her account next week. The bag of books by the door is empty again.

步骤

  1. 读一遍。用中文说主要的人做了什么。
  2. 再读一遍。每个 it、them、they 写下指什么。
  3. 回答下面三问。先写下来，再打开核对。

三个问题

三问都写在下面，才能打开核对。只留在这次浏览，不上传，也不打分。

三问都写一点，再打开核对。

再来一轮

三问写完以后，把这几个词按原句的顺序点上去。大约半分钟。排完再打开下面折着的核对。

写下以后再看核对
  • scans them：四本书；borrows it / puts it back：某本书
  • folds it / pins the receipt / It stays：收据
  • watches it：包；They walk / they decide：Qing 和 Fang

三问

  1. Qing 要还的那四本书。
  2. 罚款收据。
  3. Qing 放在椅子上的包。

给中文使用者的一处

it 在这篇里有时是收据，有时是包，有时是书。先看前面最近的单数名词。

只留一个修改点

看完核对，只写下一处下次会改的地方。

  • 如果把 “watches it” 的 it 当成了书，下次先看 leaves her bag。只改这一处。
  • 如果都对了，修改点就写：下次读到 them 或 it，仍然先找前面的名词，不改去抄生词。

两件都写，等于没有重点。只留一个。

这课不练什么

不练听，不练说，也不把这课收成单词本。写在「写」里，不在这课。
