诊所里的那一小时

技能
读
时间
约 20 分钟
做完
写明代词指什么，答完三问，并写下下一个修改点

Jun 下班去诊所，同事 Mina 也有预约。这一课还是代词，盯住 he、she、they 和 it。it 有时是血压数字，有时是一瓶水。写在这一页的框里。只留在这次浏览，不上传。

材料

这是下班后去诊所的一小时，约 250 词。不认识的词，最多查一个。不列词表，也不整篇翻译。

Jun leaves the office at 18:00 and walks to the clinic two streets away. The waiting room is quiet, and a nurse calls names from a list. Jun takes a seat near the window and checks the time on his phone. His colleague Mina arrives a few minutes later for her own appointment. She sits across from him and asks whether he feels nervous. Jun says he does not. The nurse opens the door and calls Jun's name. He stands up and follows her into a small room. She checks his blood pressure and writes it on a form. The doctor comes in, asks three short questions, and looks at the form. He says the numbers look fine for now. Jun asks when he should come back. The doctor says in three months, unless something changes. Jun thanks him and walks back to the waiting room. Mina is still there with a book. She looks up when he returns. Jun says his visit was short. Mina's name is called next, and she puts the book in her bag. She waves and follows the nurse. Jun waits outside so they can walk to the station together. When Mina comes out, they leave without hurry. At the station Jun buys a bottle of water and offers it to Mina. She takes a sip and gives it back. They board the train and talk about tomorrow's meeting for a few minutes. At Jun's stop he stands up. Mina stays on because her stop is later. He nods and steps onto the platform.

步骤

  1. 读一遍。用中文说 Jun 和 Mina 各做了什么。
  2. 再读一遍。每个 he、she、they、it 写下指什么。
  3. 回答下面三问。先写下来，再打开核对。

三个问题

三问都写在下面，才能打开核对。只留在这次浏览，不上传，也不打分。

三问都写一点，再打开核对。

再来一轮

三问写完以后，把这几个词按原句的顺序点上去。大约半分钟。排完再打开下面折着的核对。

写下以后再看核对
  • writes it on a form：血压数字（blood pressure）
  • offers it to Mina / gives it back：那瓶水
  • He stands / He says / he returns / He nods：Jun
  • She sits / She looks / She waves / she puts：Mina
  • They board / they leave：Jun 和 Mina
  • He says the numbers / thanks him：医生 / Jun 对医生

三问

  1. 血压数字（blood pressure）。
  2. 那瓶水。
  3. Jun 和 Mina。

给中文使用者的一处

it 不是人。这一篇里它有时是写在表格上的血压数字，有时是车站买的那瓶水。先看 it 前面最近的那个事物，再决定。

只留一个修改点

看完核对，只写下一处下次会改的地方。

  • 如果把 “writes it” 的 it 当成了表格，下次先看 writes 前面刚检查完的是 blood pressure。只改这一处。
  • 如果都对了，修改点就写：下次读到 it，仍然先找它前面的事物，不改去抄生词。

两件都写，等于没有重点。只留一个。

这课不练什么

不练听，不练说，也不把这课收成单词本。写在「写」里，不在这课。
