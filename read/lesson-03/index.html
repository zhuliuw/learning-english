三条工作消息

技能
读
时间
约 20 分钟
做完
写明代词指回谁，答完三问，并写下下一个修改点

午饭前，Chen Wei 改一份草稿。经理、Chen Wei、同事 Nora 是三个人，消息比三句长。这一课只做一件事：he、she、it 指回谁。不再把每句的动词都标一遍。写在这一页的框里。只留在这次浏览，不上传。

材料

这是午饭前改完草稿、再去吃面的一段，约 234 词。不认识的词，最多查一个。不列词表，也不把整篇译出来。

Before lunch, Chen Wei reads three work messages. The office is quiet, and most of the team is still in a meeting down the hall. His manager writes, "Please send the draft today." Chen Wei reads it and does not reply yet. He leaves that message on the screen and opens the draft on his desk. The draft is a short note about two client visits, and the second paragraph still looks rough. At 11:20 his teammate Nora sends a note. She says she can check the numbers. He thanks her and sends the file to her. Nora finds one wrong date. The date sits in the second paragraph, where Tuesday stands in place of the real day. She writes that the meeting is on Wednesday, not Tuesday. Chen Wei fixes the date. He reads the line twice, changes Tuesday to Wednesday, and saves the file. He sends the draft to his manager. The manager answers with one word: "Good." He does not ask for another change. Chen Wei closes the chat and goes to lunch. On the way out he tells Nora that the draft has gone. Nora nods and turns back to her own screen. Chen Wei does not take the laptop with him. He leaves the laptop closed on the desk and walks to the noodle shop on the corner. The shop is busy, and he eats without opening his phone.

步骤

  1. 读一遍。用中文说这段在讲什么。不要逐句翻译。
  2. 再读一遍。只做一件事：见到 he、she、it，写下它指回谁或什么。her 和 his 如果挡住你，可以顺手标，但不要扩成别的语法。
  3. 回答下面三问。先写下来，再打开核对。

三个问题

三问都写在下面，才能打开核对。只留在这次浏览，不上传，也不打分。

三问都写一点，再打开核对。

写下以后再看核对
  • it in “reads it”：经理那句 “Please send the draft today.”
  • He leaves that message：Chen Wei
  • She says / she can check：Nora
  • He thanks her：he 是 Chen Wei，her 是 Nora
  • She writes that the meeting：Nora
  • He reads / changes / saves：Chen Wei
  • He sends the draft：Chen Wei
  • He does not ask：经理，不是 Chen Wei
  • he tells Nora / he eats：Chen Wei

三问

  1. 经理写的那句 “Please send the draft today.”
  2. he 是 Chen Wei，her 是 Nora。
  3. 经理。

给中文使用者的一处

句子里换了一个名字，后面的 he 或 she 就从这人重新算，不要回到全文开头。reads it 里的 it 不是人，是经理那句引语。He does not ask 看它紧前面的人，那个 he 是经理，不是 Chen Wei。

只留一个修改点

看完核对，只写下一处下次会改的地方。

  • 如果把最后的 he 当成了 Chen Wei，下次先看这个 he 前面最近的人是谁。只改这一处。
  • 如果代词都对了，修改点就写：下一课仍然只问代词指回谁。

两件都写，等于没有重点。只留一个。

这课不练什么

不练听，不练说，也不把消息里的词抄进单词本。
