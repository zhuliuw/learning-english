周六的安排

技能
读
时间
约 20 分钟
做完
写明 they 和 she 指回谁，答完三问，并写下下一个修改点

从周四晚上的电话到周六下午，Mei 和弟弟 Hao 把周末过完。这一课还是只做代词。they 通常是这两个人，单独的 she 多半是 Mei，但父母来电那一处的 they 会换成别人。写在这一页的框里。只留在这次浏览，不上传。

材料

这是周四晚上的电话，加上周六从市场到 Hao 离开。约 231 词。不认识的词，最多查一个。不列词表，也不整篇翻译。

On Thursday night, Mei and her brother Hao plan Saturday. Mei calls Hao after work, and they talk while she washes rice at the sink. They do not want a long trip. The week has already been full, and a train or a hotel would take the whole day. Mei says they can meet at the market at ten. Hao agrees. He will bring a list, and she will bring cash. They will buy vegetables and cook lunch at Mei's flat. Mei names the dishes, and Hao adds eggs and tofu while they are still on the phone. Their parents sometimes call after lunch. Mei asks Hao to answer if they call, because she will be cooking. Hao says he will keep his phone near him. He will leave the phone on the table, not in his bag. On Saturday they meet at ten. Mei is already at the market gate with a cloth bag. They finish shopping before noon and walk back. The bags are heavy, so they stop once and change hands. At the flat Mei starts the rice, and Hao chops the greens. The rest of the day stays free. After lunch they sit by the window and do not plan anything else. They do not book a train or a hotel. Hao leaves before five. Mei washes the pan, checks the lock, and keeps the evening empty.

步骤

  1. 读一遍。用中文说他们打算怎么过周六。
  2. 再读一遍。每个 they、he、she 写下指回谁。同一课里 they 不必总是同一对人。
  3. 回答下面三问。先写下来，再打开核对。

三个问题

三问都写在下面，才能打开核对。只留在这次浏览，不上传，也不打分。

三问都写一点，再打开核对。

写下以后再看核对
  • they talk / They do not want / they can meet / They will buy / they are still on the phone / they meet / They finish / they stop / they sit / They do not book：Mei 和 Hao
  • she washes / she will bring / she will be cooking：Mei
  • He will bring / he will keep / He will leave：Hao
  • if they call：他们的父母

三问

  1. Mei 和 Hao。
  2. 他们的父母。
  3. Mei。

给中文使用者的一处

they 多半是 Mei 和 Hao，不要收成一个人。单独的 she 先看是不是 Mei。读到 call 时停一下：这里的 they 不一定还是这两兄妹。

只留一个修改点

看完核对，只写下一处下次会改的地方。

  • 如果两处 they 都写成了 Mei 和 Hao，下次读到 call 先停一下，看是谁会打来。只改这一处。
  • 如果都对了，修改点就写：同一个代词可以换指，下一课仍只练这一点。

两件都写，等于没有重点。只留一个。

这课不练什么

不练听，不练说，也不把买菜的词抄下来。
