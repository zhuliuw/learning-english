晚饭前的厨房

技能
读
时间
约 20 分钟
做完
写明 it 和 them 各指什么，答完三问，并写下下一个修改点

Yan 下班回家，和室友 Wei 一起做晚饭。这一课还是代词，这一次盯住 it 和 them。锅、蛋、菜、锅具都会被指回去，但不是同一个东西。写在这一页的框里。只留在这次浏览，不上传。

材料

这是下班后的一顿晚饭，约 233 词。不认识的词，最多查一个。不列词表，也不整篇翻译。

Yan comes home at 18:30 after a long day at the office. The hallway is quiet. Her roommate Wei is already in the kitchen with the radio on low. They decide to cook rice and vegetables for dinner, nothing fancy. Wei washes the vegetables in the sink and sets them aside. Yan measures the rice and puts it on. The pan is heavy. Wei lifts it and sets it on the stove. They share one cutting board near the window. Yan asks whether they still have eggs. Wei opens the fridge and finds them on the middle shelf next to the milk. She cracks two into a bowl and beats them lightly. Yan stirs the rice and watches the steam rise. When the vegetables are ready, Wei brings them to the table. Yan brings the rice in a small pot. They eat without phones and talk about the next day's work for a few minutes. After dinner, Wei washes the pans, and Yan dries them with a cloth. Wei says tomorrow she will buy more eggs on the way home. Yan nods and puts the leftover rice in a box. She leaves the box on the counter for lunch. At 21:00 they sit for a short while with tea. Then Yan goes to her room, and Wei goes to his. The kitchen light stays on until Wei turns it off before he sleeps.

步骤

  1. 读一遍。用中文说谁在厨房里做什么。
  2. 再读一遍。每个 it、them 写下指什么。she、they 可以顺手标上，但问题只考 it 和 them。
  3. 回答下面三问。先写下来，再打开核对。

三个问题

再来一轮

三问写完以后，把这几个词按原句的顺序点上去。大约半分钟。排完再打开下面折着的核对。

写下以后再看核对
  • puts it on：米
  • Wei lifts it / sets it：锅
  • sets them aside / brings them to the table：蔬菜
  • finds them / beats them：鸡蛋
  • Yan dries them：锅具
  • turns it off：厨房的灯
  • They decide / They share / They eat / they sit：Yan 和 Wei
  • She cracks / she will buy / She leaves：Wei 或 Yan，看最近的名字

三问

  1. 那口锅。
  2. 鸡蛋。
  3. 锅具。

给中文使用者的一处

them 是复数。这一篇里它有时是蔬菜，有时是鸡蛋，有时是锅具。先看前面最近的复数名词，再决定。不要把每个 them 都当成同一样东西。

只留一个修改点

看完核对，只写下一处下次会改的地方。

  • 如果把 “dries them” 的 them 当成了鸡蛋，下次先看它前面的 pans。只改这一处。
  • 如果都对了，修改点就写：下次读到 them，仍然先找前面的复数名词，不改去抄生词。

两件都写，等于没有重点。只留一个。

这课不练什么

不练听，不练说，也不把这课收成单词本。阅读到这里有六课。写在「写」里，不在这课。
