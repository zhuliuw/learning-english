父母来访

技能
读
时间
约 20 分钟
做完
写明代词指什么，答完三问，并写下下一个修改点

周末父母来住两晚，Ren 收拾房间，妹妹 Yuki 帮忙买菜。这一课盯住 it、them 和 they。行李、饭菜、钥匙都会被指回去。写在这一页的框里。只留在这次浏览，不上传。

材料

这是一篇约 240 词的短文。不认识的词，最多查一个。不列词表，也不整篇翻译。

On Friday evening, Ren clears the spare room before his parents arrive. Yuki brings two bags of groceries and sets them on the counter. They wipe the table and check the towels. Ren texts the building gate code. His father saves it on his phone. The parents arrive with a small suitcase. Ren carries it upstairs. Yuki hangs their coats and offers tea. They sit and talk about the train ride. Ren heats the soup and tastes it. Yuki plates the vegetables and passes them around. After dinner Ren shows the guest key. His mother labels it and puts it by the door. On Saturday they walk to the park nearby. Ren brings a folded umbrella and opens it once. Yuki takes photos and sends them to a cousin. In the afternoon the parents rest. Ren washes the lunch dishes and dries them. Yuki folds the blankets and stacks them. Sunday morning Ren makes eggs and shares them. His father packs the suitcase and zips it. Yuki calls a taxi and waits for it downstairs. They hug at the curb. Ren returns the guest key to the drawer. It stays there until the next visit. Yuki leaves one bag of fruit on the table. Ren puts it in a bowl. He messages that the train was on time. Yuki answers it while she rides the bus home. The spare room is quiet again.

步骤

  1. 读一遍。用中文说主要的人做了什么。
  2. 再读一遍。每个 it、them、they 写下指什么。
  3. 回答下面三问。先写下来，再打开核对。

三个问题

三问都写在下面，才能打开核对。只留在这次浏览，不上传，也不打分。

三问都写一点，再打开核对。

再来一轮

三问写完以后，把这几个词按原句的顺序点上去。大约半分钟。排完再打开下面折着的核对。

写下以后再看核对
  • saves it / carries it / tastes it / labels it / opens it / zips it / waits for it / It stays / puts it / answers it：门禁码、箱子、汤、钥匙、伞、出租车、消息、水果等
  • sets them / passes them / sends them / dries them / stacks them / shares them：购物袋／菜／照片／碗碟／毯子／鸡蛋
  • They wipe / They sit / they walk / They hug：Ren 与 Yuki／一家人

三问

  1. 父母带来的小行李箱。
  2. 两袋买来的菜／食品。
  3. Ren 发的那条消息。

给中文使用者的一处

开头的 them 指购物袋；饭桌上的 them 可能指菜。看最近的复数名词。

只留一个修改点

看完核对，只写下一处下次会改的地方。

  • 如果把 “carries it” 当成了购物袋，下次先看 a small suitcase。只改这一处。
  • 如果都对了，修改点就写：下次读到 them 或 it，仍然先找前面的名词，不改去抄生词。

两件都写，等于没有重点。只留一个。

这课不练什么

不练听，不练说，也不把这课收成单词本。写在「写」里，不在这课。
