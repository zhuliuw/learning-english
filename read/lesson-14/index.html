地铁早高峰

技能
读
时间
约 20 分钟
做完
写明代词指什么，答完三问，并写下下一个修改点

Omar 早高峰坐地铁上班，同事 Jia 在同一节车厢。这一课盯住 it、them 和 they。车厢、包、站台广播都会被指回去。写在这一页的框里。只留在这次浏览，不上传。

材料

这是一篇约 240 词的短文。不认识的词，最多查一个。不列词表，也不整篇翻译。

At seven forty, Omar swipes into the subway station. Jia waves from the platform near the map. They board the same car and hold the poles. The car fills at the next stop. Omar lifts his bag higher so it does not hit anyone. Jia checks two messages and replies to them. A recorded voice asks riders to move inside. Omar hears it twice and steps back. They reach the transfer hall after four stops. Jia points to the green line signs. Omar follows them down the stairs. On the second train a seat opens. Jia takes it and offers to hold Omar’s folder. He keeps it on his lap instead. The doors stay open longer than usual. Omar watches them and counts the seconds. Jia stands when their office stop appears. They exit with a slow crowd. Outside Omar buys a bottle of water and drinks it. Jia waits while he caps it. They walk the last block together. Omar unlocks the office gate with his card. Jia holds it open for a delivery cart. Inside they hang their coats. Omar puts his bag under the desk. It stays there until lunch. Jia sends a short note that the train was packed. Omar answers it after he opens his laptop. The morning rush is already fading outside. They start the first meeting on time.

步骤

  1. 读一遍。用中文说主要的人做了什么。
  2. 再读一遍。每个 it、them、they 写下指什么。
  3. 回答下面三问。先写下来，再打开核对。

三个问题

三问都写在下面，才能打开核对。只留在这次浏览，不上传，也不打分。

三问都写一点，再打开核对。

再来一轮

三问写完以后，把这几个词按原句的顺序点上去。大约半分钟。排完再打开下面折着的核对。

写下以后再看核对
  • so it does not hit / keeps it / drinks it / caps it / It stays / answers it：包、文件夹、水瓶、门卡、短消息等——看最近的名词
  • replies to them / follows them / watches them：消息／指示牌／车门
  • They board / They reach / They exit / They walk / They start：Omar 和 Jia

三问

  1. Omar 的包。
  2. 绿色线路的指示牌。
  3. Jia 发来的那条短消息。

给中文使用者的一处

them 可能指消息、指示牌或车门。每一次都先回到最近的复数名词。

只留一个修改点

看完核对，只写下一处下次会改的地方。

  • 如果把 “follows them” 当成了人群，下次先看 green line signs。只改这一处。
  • 如果都对了，修改点就写：下次读到 them 或 it，仍然先找前面的名词，不改去抄生词。

两件都写，等于没有重点。只留一个。

这课不练什么

不练听，不练说，也不把这课收成单词本。写在「写」里，不在这课。
