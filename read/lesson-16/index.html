周末菜市场

技能
读
时间
约 20 分钟
做完
写明代词指什么，答完三问，并写下下一个修改点

周六早上，Suki 去菜市场，摊主 Old Ma 认得她。这一课盯住 it、them 和 they。菜、袋子、找零都会被指回去。写在这一页的框里。只留在这次浏览，不上传。

材料

这是一篇约 240 词的短文。不认识的词，最多查一个。不列词表，也不整篇翻译。

On Saturday morning, Suki walks to the wet market with two cloth bags. Old Ma waves from the greens stall. They talk about the rain while he weighs spinach. Suki points to the tomatoes and asks for six. He picks them and sets them in a bowl. She pays and drops the change into her pocket. It jingles when she walks. At the fish stall a seller wraps two fillets. Suki smells them and nods. She puts them in the colder bag. Old Ma holds a bunch of scallions for her. She takes it and thanks him. They finish before the aisle grows crowded. Suki checks her list and crosses items off. She folds it into the bag. A neighbor asks where the tofu is. Suki points and walks with her. They buy one block and split it at home later. Outside Suki balances both bags on her bike. One strap slips, so she tightens it. Old Ma calls that the spinach is especially fresh. Suki waves and rides home. In the kitchen she rinses the greens and drains them. She sets the fish in the fridge and closes it. The list on the counter is blank now. She throws it away after dinner planning. Her roommate asks about the tomatoes. Suki says they will go into a salad. She washes her hands and hangs the bags to dry. They drip on the balcony for an hour.

步骤

  1. 读一遍。用中文说主要的人做了什么。
  2. 再读一遍。每个 it、them、they 写下指什么。
  3. 回答下面三问。先写下来，再打开核对。

三个问题

三问都写在下面，才能打开核对。只留在这次浏览，不上传，也不打分。

三问都写一点，再打开核对。

再来一轮

三问写完以后，把这几个词按原句的顺序点上去。大约半分钟。排完再打开下面折着的核对。

写下以后再看核对
  • It jingles / takes it / folds it / tightens it / closes it / throws it：找零、葱、清单、带子、冰箱、清单
  • picks them / smells them / puts them / drains them / They drip：番茄／鱼／菜／袋子
  • They talk / They finish / They buy / they will go：Suki 与 Old Ma／邻居；或西红柿

三问

  1. 口袋里的找零。
  2. 两片鱼肉。
  3. 西红柿。

给中文使用者的一处

最后一个 they 指西红柿，不是人。看 go into a salad。

只留一个修改点

看完核对，只写下一处下次会改的地方。

  • 如果把最后的 they 当成了室友，下次先看 asks about the tomatoes。只改这一处。
  • 如果都对了，修改点就写：下次读到 them 或 it，仍然先找前面的名词，不改去抄生词。

两件都写，等于没有重点。只留一个。

这课不练什么

不练听，不练说，也不把这课收成单词本。写在「写」里，不在这课。
