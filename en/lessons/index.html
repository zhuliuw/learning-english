Lessons already here

Lesson index

Only the forty-eight lessons already here, for adults who use Chinese. The three-month path is on three months: each week, one listening, one reading, one speaking, and one writing lesson. Listening, speaking, and writing take about fifteen minutes. Reading takes about twenty. Month 1 uses lessons 1–4; month 2 uses lessons 5–8; month 3 uses lessons 9–12.

How the three months are laid out

Month 1

  • Week 1: listening and speaking 1; reading and writing 1.
  • Week 2: listening and speaking 2; reading and writing 2.
  • Week 3: listening and speaking 3; reading and writing 3.
  • Week 4: listening and speaking 4; reading and writing 4.

Month 2

  • Week 1: listening and speaking 5; reading and writing 5.
  • Week 2: listening and speaking 6; reading and writing 6.
  • Week 3: listening and speaking 7; reading and writing 7.
  • Week 4: listening and speaking 8; reading and writing 8.

Month 3

  • Week 1: listening and speaking 9; reading and writing 9.
  • Week 2: listening and speaking 10; reading and writing 10.
  • Week 3: listening and speaking 11; reading and writing 11.
  • Week 4: listening and speaking 12; reading and writing 12.
