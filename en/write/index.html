Writing · twenty-four lessons

Writing

Twenty-four lessons. Each changes one line you already read: your own day, time, place, or method—a deadline, leaving home, moving a meeting, a meeting place, leaving work, getting home, a clinic visit, a water cut, the bank, a late shift, returning books, a delivery, the community clinic, the morning rush, a drill, the market, a repair drop-off, signing up for an outing, renewing a lease, a car inspection, clearing a spare room, leaving for a wedding, annual leave, and the year-end review. About fifteen minutes. Write in the box on the lesson page. The sample stays folded; it is someone else’s detail, about forty to sixty words, for comparison—do not copy it. Check four points; this page does not score. After you write your sentence, you can tap five words from the line back into order—about half a minute. On the six-month path, do one lesson each week. Lessons 1–4 go in month 1; 5–8 in month 2; 9–12 in month 3; 13–16 in month 4; 17–20 in month 5; 21–24 in month 6.
