Writing · twelve lessons

Writing

Twelve lessons. Each one changes a sentence you already read: your deadline, the time you leave, the day a meeting moves, where and when you meet, the time you leave the office, the time you get home, the time you leave for the clinic, the day water stops, the time you leave for the bank, the time a late shift ends, the day you return books, or the evening you pick up a delivery. About fifteen minutes. Write in the box on the lesson page. The sample stays folded; it is someone else’s detail, about forty to sixty words, for comparison—do not copy it. Four checks, no score on the page. After you write your sentence, you can put five words from the line back in order. About half a minute. On the three-month path, do one each week. Lessons 1–4 are in month 1; lessons 5–8 are in month 2; lessons 9–12 are in month 3.
