Writing · four lessons

Writing

Four lessons. Each one changes a sentence you have already read: your own deadline, the time you leave, the day a meeting moves, or the place and time you meet. About fifteen minutes. Write in the box on the lesson page. The model stays folded. It is someone else’s detail, about forty to sixty words, there to compare with, so leave it uncopied. Four checks, and this page does not score you. After you write your sentence, you can put five words from that line back in order. About half a minute. Do one of these this week.

  • Lesson 1 · one sentence · about 15 minutesYour own deadline
  • Lesson 2 · one sentence · about 15 minutesThe time you leave
  • Lesson 3 · one sentence · about 15 minutesThe day the meeting moves
  • Lesson 4 · one sentence · about 15 minutesWhere and when you meet

Write it in the box on the lesson page. There is no score, and nobody marks your sentence. Do not do two writing lessons in one day.
