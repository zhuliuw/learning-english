Writing · Lesson 1 · about 15 minutes

Your own deadline

Skill
Writing
Time
About 15 minutes
Done when
You have rewritten one sentence on paper and checked the four items

The sentence comes from a passage you have already read. It is not a new topic. Write on paper, or in your own notes. This site does not receive your text, and it does not score it.

Material

Change only this sentence. Replace Thursday with your own deadline. Make the person yourself. Do not keep Lin Wei’s Thursday.

She will finish the report on Thursday, not today.

The sentence is in reading lesson 1. Open that lesson only if you need the passage again.

Steps

  1. Read the sentence above. Do not open the model yet.
  2. Write one English sentence on paper. Use only your own detail.
  3. Then open the model. It is someone else’s detail, about forty to sixty words. Do not copy it.
  4. Check the sentence on your paper against the list. This page has no score. To keep the page, use the browser’s print. Open the model first if you want it on the page.
Open the model after you write

I write a short note for my team. The note is a report about this week's visits. I will finish the report on Monday, not Friday. I send it to my manager before noon. Monday is my deadline. I do not promise the report today. I use today for the three messages I already answered.

This is an example, not your answer.

Check

Use only these four items. Do not switch to grammar terms, and do not give yourself a score.

  1. Is it clear who does it?
  2. Is the action clear?
  3. Is the time clear?
  4. Is the sentence finished?

Keep one change

From the four items, write down the one you missed. If you passed all four, write this: next lesson, still check only these four.

Writing all four means you have no priority. Keep one.
