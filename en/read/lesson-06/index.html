The kitchen before dinner

Skill
Reading
Time
About 20 minutes
Done when
You have written what it and them point to, answered three questions, and written one change

Yan comes home from work and cooks dinner with her roommate Wei. This lesson is still pronouns. Watch it and them. The pan, the eggs, the vegetables, and the pans can all be pointed back to, but they are not the same thing. Write in the box on this page. It stays in this browser for this visit, and it is not uploaded.

Passage

This is dinner after work, about 233 words. Look up at most one word. There is no word list, and do not translate the whole passage.

Yan comes home at 18:30 after a long day at the office. The hallway is quiet. Her roommate Wei is already in the kitchen with the radio on low. They decide to cook rice and vegetables for dinner, nothing fancy. Wei washes the vegetables in the sink and sets them aside. Yan measures the rice and puts it on. The pan is heavy. Wei lifts it and sets it on the stove. They share one cutting board near the window. Yan asks whether they still have eggs. Wei opens the fridge and finds them on the middle shelf next to the milk. She cracks two into a bowl and beats them lightly. Yan stirs the rice and watches the steam rise. When the vegetables are ready, Wei brings them to the table. Yan brings the rice in a small pot. They eat without phones and talk about the next day's work for a few minutes. After dinner, Wei washes the pans, and Yan dries them with a cloth. Wei says tomorrow she will buy more eggs on the way home. Yan nods and puts the leftover rice in a box. She leaves the box on the counter for lunch. At 21:00 they sit for a short while with tea. Then Yan goes to her room, and Wei goes to his. The kitchen light stays on until Wei turns it off before he sleeps.

Steps

  1. Read once. Say in Chinese who does what in the kitchen.
  2. Read again. For each it and them, write what it points to. You can mark she and they too, but the questions ask only about it and them.
  3. Answer the three questions below. Write first, then open the check.

The questions

  1. The pan.
  2. The eggs.
  3. The pans.

One point for a Chinese speaker

them is plural. In this passage it is sometimes the vegetables, sometimes the eggs, and sometimes the pans. Look at the nearest plural noun before it. Do not make every them the same thing.

Keep one change

After you check, write down one thing you will do differently.

  • If you took them in “dries them” to be the eggs, next time look at the pans just before it. Change only that.
  • If they were right, write this: next time you meet them, still find the plural noun just before it, and do not switch to copying words.

Writing both means you have no priority. Keep one.
