Reading · Lesson 1 · about 15 minutes

Find who does what

Skill
Reading
Time
About 15 minutes
Done when
You have answered three questions and written one change

This lesson practices one move: in each sentence, mark who, and mark the verb. Write on paper or in your own notes. This page does not save what you write.

Passage

The passage is short and the words are common on purpose. It is easy so you can see what you are practicing. Mark who and the verb even if you already know every sentence.

Lin Wei leaves home at 7:10. She takes the bus and reads three short messages on the way. She does not answer them yet. At the office she writes one reply before she opens her email. She sends that reply to her manager. She will finish the report on Thursday, not today.

Look up at most one word.

Steps

  1. Read once without stopping or looking words up. Then say in Chinese whose situation this is. If you cannot say it cleanly, continue anyway.
  2. Read again. In each sentence, mark who and the verb. If the verb includes not or will, mark that whole piece; do not split it. The second sentence has two verbs, takes and reads. Mark both. In the fourth sentence, mark both writes and opens.
  3. Answer the three questions. Write them down before you open the check.

Three questions

  1. What does Lin Wei read on the bus, and does she reply then?
  2. Where does she write the reply, and who is it for?
  3. When will she finish the report?
Write on paper first, then check

Who / verb

  • Lin Wei / leaves
  • She / takes, She / reads
  • She / does not answer
  • she / writes, she / opens
  • She / sends
  • She / will finish

The questions

  1. She reads three short messages and does not reply yet.
  2. At the office, before she opens her email. She sends it to her manager.
  3. On Thursday, not today.

Keep one change

After you check, write down one thing you will do differently next time.

  • If you missed a time, next time put a dot under a weekday, today, or tomorrow. Change only that.
  • If you missed the second verb in sentence two, next time mark both actions when and joins them.
  • If the questions and the verbs were all right, write this: next lesson, still find who does what, and do not add a new job.

Writing down all three means you have no priority. Keep one.

What this lesson does not practice

Not pronunciation, not listening, and not copying these six sentences into a word list. Stop when you finish. The next lesson is the early bus, and it still only asks who does what.
