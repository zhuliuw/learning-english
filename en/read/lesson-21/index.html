Reading · Lesson 21 · about 20 minutes

When parents visit

Skill
Reading
Time
About 20 minutes
Done when
You have written what the pronouns point to, answered three questions, and written one change

Parents visit for two nights; Ren tidies the flat, and sister Yuki helps with groceries. Track it, them, and they. Bags, meals, and keys are pointed back to. Write in the box on this page. It stays in this browser for this visit, and it is not uploaded.

Material

About 240 words. Look up at most one unknown word. There is no word list and no full translation.

On Friday evening, Ren clears the spare room before his parents arrive. Yuki brings two bags of groceries and sets them on the counter. They wipe the table and check the towels. Ren texts the building gate code. His father saves it on his phone. The parents arrive with a small suitcase. Ren carries it upstairs. Yuki hangs their coats and offers tea. They sit and talk about the train ride. Ren heats the soup and tastes it. Yuki plates the vegetables and passes them around. After dinner Ren shows the guest key. His mother labels it and puts it by the door. On Saturday they walk to the park nearby. Ren brings a folded umbrella and opens it once. Yuki takes photos and sends them to a cousin. In the afternoon the parents rest. Ren washes the lunch dishes and dries them. Yuki folds the blankets and stacks them. Sunday morning Ren makes eggs and shares them. His father packs the suitcase and zips it. Yuki calls a taxi and waits for it downstairs. They hug at the curb. Ren returns the guest key to the drawer. It stays there until the next visit. Yuki leaves one bag of fruit on the table. Ren puts it in a bowl. He messages that the train was on time. Yuki answers it while she rides the bus home. The spare room is quiet again.

Steps

  1. Read once. In Chinese, say what the main people did.
  2. Read again. For each it, them, and they, write what it points to.
  3. Answer the three questions below. Write first, then open the check.

Three questions

Write all three below before you open the check. It stays in this browser for this visit. It is not uploaded, and it is not scored.

Write a little for all three, then open the check.

One more round

After the three answers, tap these words back into the order of the line. About half a minute. Finish before you open the check.

Open after you write
  • saves it / carries it / tastes it / labels it / opens it / zips it / waits for it / It stays / puts it / answers it: gate code, suitcase, soup, key, umbrella, taxi, message, fruit, and so on
  • sets them / passes them / sends them / dries them / stacks them / shares them: bags / vegetables / photos / dishes / blankets / eggs
  • They wipe / They sit / they walk / They hug: Ren and Yuki / the family

Three questions

  1. The small suitcase.
  2. The two grocery bags.
  3. Ren’s message.

One note for Chinese speakers

Early them points to grocery bags; at the table it may point to vegetables. Use the nearest plural noun.

Keep one change

After the check, write only one thing to change next time.

  • If you took “carries it” as the grocery bags, next time look at a small suitcase. Change only that.
  • If they were right, write this: next time you see them or it, still find the noun first, and do not switch to copying new words.

Writing both means no focus. Keep one.

What this lesson skips
