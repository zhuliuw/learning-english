Leaving a laptop for repair

Skill
Reading
Time
About 20 minutes
Done when
You have written what the pronouns point to, answered three questions, and written one change

Leo leaves a laptop at a repair shop; clerk Fran writes the ticket. Track it, them, and they. Machines, parts, and receipts are pointed back to. Write in the box on this page. It stays in this browser for this visit, and it is not uploaded.

Material

About 240 words. Look up at most one unknown word. There is no word list and no full translation.

After lunch, Leo carries his laptop to the repair shop on the corner. Fran greets him and opens a ticket on the screen. Leo explains that it freezes when two apps run. Fran asks him to show the error. He turns it on and waits. The screen locks, and Fran copies the code. She writes two possible causes and underlines them. Leo leaves the charger and a note with his number. Fran tags them together in a plastic tray. She says the check will take two days. Leo keeps the claim slip in his wallet. It sits next to his transit card. Two days later he returns and rings the bell. Fran brings the laptop and a small box of parts. She shows them before she closes the case. Leo tests the apps and nods. He pays and asks for a paper receipt. Fran prints it and signs the bottom. Outside Leo texts his teammate that the machine is back. He sends a photo of the receipt with it. On the bus he opens a document and saves it. The fan stays quiet. At the office Fran’s note still lists the two causes. Leo files them in a folder labeled repair. His manager asks whether the delay affected the draft. Leo says they finished it on a shared computer. He puts the laptop on the dock and locks it. The claim slip goes into the same folder. It will stay there until the warranty ends. Leo thanks Fran in a short review after work.

Steps

  1. Read once. In Chinese, say what the main people did.
  2. Read again. For each it, them, and they, write what it points to.
  3. Answer the three questions below. Write first, then open the check.

Three questions

Write all three below before you open the check. It stays in this browser for this visit. It is not uploaded, and it is not scored.

Write a little for all three, then open the check.

One more round

After the three answers, tap these words back into the order of the line. About half a minute. Finish before you open the check.

Open after you write
  • it freezes / turns it on / keeps It / prints it / sends … with it / saves it / locks it / It will stay: laptop, slip, receipt, document, and so on
  • underlines them / tags them / shows them / files them: causes / charger and note / parts / cause notes
  • they finished: Leo and his teammate

Three questions

  1. His laptop.
  2. The charger and the note with his number.
  3. Leo and his teammate (they).

One note for Chinese speakers

The first them may be the two causes; later ones may be the charger and note. Use the nearest plural noun.

Keep one change

After the check, write only one thing to change next time.

  • If you took “tags them” as the two causes, next time look at leaves the charger and a note. Change only that.
  • If they were right, write this: next time you see them or it, still find the noun first, and do not switch to copying new words.

Writing both means no focus. Keep one.

What this lesson skips
