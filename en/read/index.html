Reading · twelve lessons

Reading

Twelve longer passages: the Thursday report, the early bus, a few work messages before lunch, a weekend, a late train, the kitchen before dinner, an hour at the clinic, a hallway water notice, an hour at the bank, a late supermarket shift, returning books at the library, and a lobby delivery. The kind of day you already have. The first two ask you to find who does what. The next ten ask what he, she, they, it, and them point to. About twenty minutes. Look up one word at most. No word list, and no full translation. After the three answers, you can put four or five words from a line back in order. About half a minute. On the three-month path, do one each week. Lessons 1–4 are in month 1; lessons 5–8 are in month 2; lessons 9–12 are in month 3.
