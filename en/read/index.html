Reading · five lessons

Reading

Five longer passages: the Thursday report, the early bus, a few work messages before lunch, a weekend, and a late train. The kind of day you already have. The first two ask you to find who does what. The next three ask what he, she, they, and it point back to. About twenty minutes, and one lookup at most. No word list, and you do not translate the whole passage. After the three answers, you can put four or five words from one line back in order. About half a minute. Do one of these this week.

  • Lesson 1 · who does what · about 20 minutes Find who does what
  • Lesson 2 · still who does what · about 20 minutes The early bus
  • Lesson 3 · what a pronoun points to · about 20 minutes Three work messages
  • Lesson 4 · what a pronoun points to · about 20 minutes Saturday’s plan
  • Lesson 5 · what a pronoun points to · about 20 minutes The late train
