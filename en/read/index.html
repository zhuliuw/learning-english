Reading · twenty-four lessons

Reading

Twenty-four slightly longer passages: a Thursday report, the early bus, work messages, a weekend, a delayed train, the kitchen, a clinic hour, a water notice, the bank, a late store shift, the library, a lobby delivery, the community clinic, the morning subway, a fire drill, the wet market, laptop repair, a team outing, renewing a lease, a car inspection, parents visiting, a coworker’s wedding, annual leave, and the year-end review. All are things adults meet after work starts. The first two lessons only find who does what. The later twenty-two track what he, she, they, it, and them point back to. About twenty minutes a lesson; look up at most one word. No word list and no full translation. After the three answers, you can tap four or five words back into order—about half a minute. On the six-month path, do one lesson each week. Lessons 1–4 go in month 1; 5–8 in month 2; 9–12 in month 3; 13–16 in month 4; 17–20 in month 5; 21–24 in month 6.
