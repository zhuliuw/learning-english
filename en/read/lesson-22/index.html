A coworker’s wedding

Skill
Reading
Time
About 20 minutes
Done when
You have written what the pronouns point to, answered three questions, and written one change

On Saturday a coworker gets married; Ava attends, and Dom shares her table. Track it, them, and they. Invites, gifts, and seats are pointed back to. Write in the box on this page. It stays in this browser for this visit, and it is not uploaded.

Material

About 240 words. Look up at most one unknown word. There is no word list and no full translation.

On Saturday afternoon, Ava pins the wedding invite to her fridge one last time. She folds it into her bag before she leaves. Dom meets her at the hotel lobby. They find their names on the seating board. Ava carries a wrapped gift and sets it on the gift table. A staff member moves the gifts and stacks them carefully. Dom takes two programs and hands one to Ava. She reads it during the short wait. The ceremony starts on time. They stand when the couple enters. Ava wipes her eyes once and laughs about it later. After the vows Dom leads them to table nine. Ava hangs her coat on the chair and smooths it. Servers bring dishes and refill them through the meal. Ava tastes the soup and nods. Dom takes photos and sends them to the team chat. Ava likes the clearest one and saves it. Near the end they sign a small guest book. Ava writes a short line and dots it. Dom adds his name under hers. Outside they wait for a taxi in the cool air. Ava holds the program so it does not blow away. Dom opens the car door and closes it after her. At home Ava hangs the invite on a board. It stays there until she files the photos. Dom messages that the gift table was full. Ava answers it before she sleeps. On Monday they tell coworkers the day went well. Ava returns the spare umbrella she borrowed. Dom puts it back by the office door.

Steps

  1. Read once. In Chinese, say what the main people did.
  2. Read again. For each it, them, and they, write what it points to.
  3. Answer the three questions below. Write first, then open the check.

One more round

After the three answers, tap these words back into the order of the line. About half a minute. Finish before you open the check.

Open after you write
  • folds it / sets it / reads it / about it / smooths it / saves it / dots it / so it does not / closes it / It stays / answers it / puts it: invite, gift, program, coat, photo, line, door, message, umbrella, and so on
  • stacks them / refill them / sends them / leads them: gifts / dishes / photos / the two to the table
  • They find / They stand / they sign / they wait / they tell: Ava and Dom

Three questions

  1. The wedding invite.
  2. The gifts on the gift table.
  3. The borrowed umbrella.

One note for Chinese speakers

The last it points to the umbrella, not the invite. Look at returns the spare umbrella.

Keep one change

After the check, write only one thing to change next time.

  • If you took the last it as the invite, next time look at returns the spare umbrella. Change only that.
  • If they were right, write this: next time you see them or it, still find the noun first, and do not switch to copying new words.

Writing both means no focus. Keep one.
