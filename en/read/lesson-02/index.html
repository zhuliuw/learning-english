The early bus

Skill
Reading
Time
About 20 minutes
Done when
You have marked who and the verb, answered three questions, and written one change

Gao Lin leaves just after seven, rides the number 12 bus, and buys a coffee after he steps off. A woman types beside him, and a driver is at the wheel. Same job as lesson 1: find who does what. Do not chase pronouns yet. Write in the box on this page. It stays in this browser for this visit, and it is not uploaded.

Passage

This is the commute from the apartment door to the first file of the day, about 262 words. The words are common. Look up at most one word. There is no word list, and there is no translation.

Gao Lin leaves his apartment at 7:05. He checks the stove, picks up his keys, and puts a notebook into his bag. He locks the door and walks to the bus stop. The pavement is wet, and two other people already wait under the shelter. The number 12 bus arrives at 7:20. A few passengers step off, and then the line moves forward. Gao Lin shows his card and sits by the window. He sets his bag on his lap and loosens his coat. A woman beside him opens a laptop and types a short reply. She does not look up, and she does not turn the screen toward him. The driver waits at a red light and then turns onto the main road. The bus moves slowly behind a line of cars. Gao Lin does not speak. He watches the stops and checks his watch. A man near the door talks on a phone, but Gao Lin does not join in. He counts the stops he knows: the river, the school, the market, and then his building. At 7:48 the bus stops outside his building. People stand before the doors open. Gao Lin thanks the driver and steps off. The air outside is colder than the bus. He buys a coffee in the shop by the door. The person at the counter already knows the order and slides the cup across. He carries the coffee upstairs and turns on his computer before eight. He sets the cup down, opens the first file of the day, and does not go back downstairs.

Steps

  1. Read once without stopping. Say in Chinese whose situation this is. Continue even if that summary is rough.
  2. Read again. In each sentence, mark who and the verb. If the verb includes not or will, mark that whole piece. If and joins two actions, mark both.
  3. Answer the three questions. Write them down before you open the check.

One point for a Chinese speaker

Two people share the scene. Gao Lin’s verbs are his. The woman beside him types; those verbs are not his. thanks the driver is still person before verb: Gao Lin thanks the driver.

Keep one change

After you check, write down one thing you will do differently.

  • If you missed the second verb after and, mark both next time. Change only that.
  • If the people and the verbs were right, write this: next lesson, add only what a pronoun points to, and do not add another job.

Writing both means you have no priority. Keep one.

What this lesson does not practice
