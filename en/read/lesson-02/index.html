Reading · Lesson 2 · about 15 minutes

The early bus

Skill
Reading
Time
About 15 minutes
Done when
You have marked who and the verb, answered three questions, and written one change

This lesson practices the same one thing as lesson 1: find who does what. It checks that the method still works on another passage. Do not chase what a pronoun points to yet. Write on paper. This page does not save what you write.

Passage

This is a commute to work. The words are common. Look up at most one word. There is no word list, and there is no translation.

Gao Lin leaves his apartment at 7:05. He locks the door and walks to the bus stop. The number 12 bus arrives at 7:20. Gao Lin shows his card and sits by the window. A woman beside him opens a laptop and types a short reply. The driver waits at a red light and then turns onto the main road. Gao Lin does not speak. He watches the stops and checks his watch. At 7:48 the bus stops outside his building. Gao Lin thanks the driver and steps off. He buys a coffee in the shop by the door. He carries the coffee upstairs and turns on his computer before eight.

Steps

  1. Read once without stopping. Say in Chinese whose situation this is. Continue even if that summary is rough.
  2. Read again. In each sentence, mark who and the verb. If the verb includes not or will, mark that whole piece. If and joins two actions, mark both.
  3. Answer the three questions. Write them down before you open the check.

Three questions

  1. What does the woman beside Gao Lin do on the bus?
  2. Does Gao Lin speak on the bus?
  3. What does he do after he steps off?
Write on paper first, then check

Who / verb

  • Gao Lin / leaves
  • He / locks, He / walks
  • The number 12 bus / arrives
  • Gao Lin / shows, sits
  • A woman / opens, types
  • The driver / waits, turns
  • Gao Lin / does not speak
  • He / watches, checks
  • the bus / stops
  • Gao Lin / thanks, steps
  • He / buys
  • He / carries, turns

The questions

  1. She opens a laptop and types a short reply.
  2. No.
  3. He buys a coffee in the shop by the door, carries the coffee upstairs, and turns on his computer before eight.

Keep one change

After you check, write down one thing you will do differently.

  • If you missed the second verb after and, mark both next time. Change only that.
  • If the people and the verbs were right, write this: next lesson, add only what a pronoun points to, and do not add another job.

Writing both means you have no priority. Keep one.

What this lesson does not practice

Not listening, not speaking, and not copying new words into a list. Pronouns start in the next lesson.
