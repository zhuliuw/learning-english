The yearly car inspection

Skill
Reading
Time
About 20 minutes
Done when
You have written what the pronouns point to, answered three questions, and written one change

Zoe drives to the inspection station for the yearly check; inspector Han waits at a bay. Track it, them, and they. Cars, forms, and stickers are pointed back to. Write in the box on this page. It stays in this browser for this visit, and it is not uploaded.

Material

About 240 words. Look up at most one unknown word. There is no word list and no full translation.

On a clear Monday, Zoe drives to the inspection station before nine. Han waves her into bay two. They start with the lights and the horn. Zoe turns them on when Han asks. He checks the tires and measures them. Zoe hands over the registration and waits. Han scans it and prints a checklist. She keeps a copy on the dashboard. It slides once, so she clips it. A second inspector joins and reviews the brakes. Han explains two small fixes and points to them. Zoe writes them in her phone notes. She pays the fee at the window. The clerk gives her a sticker and a receipt. Zoe peels the sticker and presses it on the glass. Han stamps the form and returns it. Outside Zoe texts her partner that the car passed. She sends a photo of the sticker with it. On the way home she buys wiper fluid and installs it. The checklist stays in the glove box. Zoe files the receipt in a sleeve. It sits with last year’s papers. Han’s notes about the fixes remain on her phone. She schedules them for the next Saturday. Her partner asks whether the line was long. Zoe says they finished in forty minutes. She washes her hands and locks the car. The new sticker catches the afternoon light. Zoe checks it once more before she walks inside. The yearly inspection is done.

Steps

  1. Read once. In Chinese, say what the main people did.
  2. Read again. For each it, them, and they, write what it points to.
  3. Answer the three questions below. Write first, then open the check.

One more round

After the three answers, tap these words back into the order of the line. About half a minute. Finish before you open the check.

Open after you write
  • scans it / keeps It / clips it / returns it / with it / installs it / files It / checks it: registration, checklist, form, text, fluid, receipt, sticker
  • turns them / measures them / points to them / writes them / schedules them: lights and horn / tires / two small fixes
  • They start / they finished: Zoe and Han / the people in the check

Three questions

  1. The lights and the horn.
  2. The checklist copy.
  3. The text (sent with the photo).

One note for Chinese speakers

In with it, it points to the text; the sticker is what the photo shows. Look back to texts her partner.

Keep one change

After the check, write only one thing to change next time.

  • If you took “with it” as the sticker, next time look at texts … that the car passed. Change only that.
  • If they were right, write this: next time you see them or it, still find the noun first, and do not switch to copying new words.

Writing both means no focus. Keep one.
