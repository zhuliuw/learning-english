Reading · Lesson 8 · about 20 minutes

The notice in the hallway

Skill
Reading
Time
About 20 minutes
Done when
You have marked what each pronoun points to, answered three questions, and written one change

Rui finds a water-cut notice in the hallway; his neighbor Lena prepares with him. Track it and them. The notice, the photo, the bottles, and the pots are all pointed back to, but not as the same thing. Write in the box on this page. It stays in this browser for this visit, and it is not uploaded.

Material

A day when the building water stops, about 240 words. Look up at most one unknown word. There is no word list and no full translation.

On Thursday evening, Rui finds a notice taped to the hallway wall. The notice says the water will stop on Saturday from nine to noon. Rui reads it twice and takes a photo with his phone. His neighbor Lena opens her door and asks what it says. Rui shows her the photo. She reads it and nods. They decide to fill bottles on Friday night. Rui buys six bottles at the corner shop after work. He leaves them by the kitchen door. Lena fills two pots and sets them on the counter. On Saturday morning the taps are dry. Rui makes coffee with water from a bottle. Lena washes fruit with water from a pot. At eleven they hear workers in the basement. The noise stops just before noon. Rui tries the kitchen tap. It runs again, a little cloudy at first. He lets it run for a minute, then fills the kettle. Lena knocks and asks whether the water is back. Rui says yes and offers her a spare bottle. She takes it and says she will return the pots to the shelf. They put the empty bottles in a bag for the recycling bin. Rui takes the bag downstairs. Lena holds the door for him. In the evening Rui peels the notice off the wall because the work is done. He folds it and drops it in the paper bin. The hallway is quiet again.

Steps

  1. Read once. Say in Chinese what the notice says, and what Rui and Lena each do.
  2. Read again. For each it and them, write what it points to.
  3. Answer the three questions below. Write first, then open the check.

After you write, open the check
  • what it says / reads it: the hallway notice

  • sets them on the counter / return the pots: the two pots
  • She takes it: the spare bottle
  • tries the kitchen tap / It runs: the water from the tap
  • They decide / they hear: Rui and Lena

The questions

  1. The hallway notice.
  2. The six bottles.
  3. The spare bottle.

One point for a Chinese speaker

them is plural. In this passage it is sometimes the bottles and sometimes the pots. Look at the nearest plural noun before it. Do not make every them the same thing.

Keep one change

After you check, write down one thing you will do differently.

  • If you took them in “leaves them” to be the pots, next time look at the six bottles just bought. Change only that.
  • If they were right, write this: next time you meet them, still find the plural noun just before it, and do not switch to copying words.

Writing both means you have no priority. Keep one.
