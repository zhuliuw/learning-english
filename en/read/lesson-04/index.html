Reading · Lesson 4 · about 15 minutes

Saturday’s plan

Skill
Reading
Time
About 15 minutes
Done when
You have written what they and she point to, answered three questions, and written one change

This lesson is still only pronouns. The point is that they can change who it means. Write on paper. This page does not save what you write.

Passage

Two adults are planning a weekend. Look up at most one word. There is no word list, and do not translate the whole passage.

On Thursday night, Mei and her brother Hao plan Saturday. They do not want a long trip. Mei says they can meet at the market at ten. Hao agrees. He will bring a list, and she will bring cash. They will buy vegetables and cook lunch at Mei's flat. Their parents sometimes call after lunch. Mei asks Hao to answer if they call, because she will be cooking. Hao says he will keep his phone near him. On Saturday they meet at ten. They finish shopping before noon and walk back. The rest of the day stays free. They do not book a train or a hotel.

Steps

  1. Read once. Say in Chinese how they plan to spend Saturday.
  2. Read again. For each they, he, and she, write who it points back to. They does not have to be the same pair every time.
  3. Answer the three questions. Write them down before you open the check.

Three questions

  1. In “They do not want a long trip”, who are they?
  2. In “answer if they call”, who are they?
  3. In “she will be cooking”, who is she?
Write on paper first, then check
  • They do not want / they can meet / They will buy / they meet / They finish / They do not book: Mei and Hao
  • He will bring / he will keep: Hao
  • she will bring / she will be cooking: Mei
  • if they call: their parents

The questions

  1. Mei and Hao.
  2. Their parents.
  3. Mei.

Keep one change

After you check, write down one thing you will do differently.

  • If both uses of they became Mei and Hao, next time pause at call and see who might phone. Change only that.
  • If they were right, write this: the same pronoun can switch, and the next lesson still practices only that.

Writing both means you have no priority. Keep one.

What this lesson does not practice

Not listening, not speaking, and not copying the shopping words into a list.
