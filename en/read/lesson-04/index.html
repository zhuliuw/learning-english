Saturday’s plan

Skill
Reading
Time
About 20 minutes
Done when
You have written what they and she point to, answered three questions, and written one change

From a Thursday-night call to Saturday afternoon, Mei and her brother Hao get through the weekend. This lesson is still only pronouns. they is usually the two of them, and she alone is often Mei, but the they on the parents’ call is someone else. Write in the box on this page. It stays in this browser for this visit, and it is not uploaded.

Passage

This is the Thursday call and the Saturday from the market until Hao leaves. About 231 words. Look up at most one word. There is no word list, and do not translate the whole passage.

On Thursday night, Mei and her brother Hao plan Saturday. Mei calls Hao after work, and they talk while she washes rice at the sink. They do not want a long trip. The week has already been full, and a train or a hotel would take the whole day. Mei says they can meet at the market at ten. Hao agrees. He will bring a list, and she will bring cash. They will buy vegetables and cook lunch at Mei's flat. Mei names the dishes, and Hao adds eggs and tofu while they are still on the phone. Their parents sometimes call after lunch. Mei asks Hao to answer if they call, because she will be cooking. Hao says he will keep his phone near him. He will leave the phone on the table, not in his bag. On Saturday they meet at ten. Mei is already at the market gate with a cloth bag. They finish shopping before noon and walk back. The bags are heavy, so they stop once and change hands. At the flat Mei starts the rice, and Hao chops the greens. The rest of the day stays free. After lunch they sit by the window and do not plan anything else. They do not book a train or a hotel. Hao leaves before five. Mei washes the pan, checks the lock, and keeps the evening empty.

Steps

  1. Read once. Say in Chinese how they plan to spend Saturday.
  2. Read again. For each they, he, and she, write who it points back to. They does not have to be the same pair every time.
  3. Answer the three questions. Write them down before you open the check.

After you write, open the check
  • they talk / They do not want / they can meet / They will buy / they are still on the phone / they meet / They finish / they stop / they sit / They do not book: Mei and Hao
  • she washes / she will bring / she will be cooking: Mei
  • He will bring / he will keep / He will leave: Hao
  • if they call: their parents

The questions

  1. Mei and Hao.
  2. Their parents.
  3. Mei.

One point for a Chinese speaker

they is usually Mei and Hao. Do not collapse they into one person. she alone is often Mei. At call, stop: that they may not be the brother and sister.

Keep one change

After you check, write down one thing you will do differently.

  • If both uses of they became Mei and Hao, next time pause at call and see who might phone. Change only that.
  • If they were right, write this: the same pronoun can switch, and the next lesson still practices only that.

Writing both means you have no priority. Keep one.

What this lesson does not practice

Not listening, not speaking, and not copying the shopping words into a list.
