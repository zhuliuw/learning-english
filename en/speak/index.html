Speaking

For adults who use Chinese. Four lessons, the lines you already heard, no new topic. About fifteen minutes: listen once, open the lines, say them twice, then record once on your phone. The recording stays on the phone. This site does not receive it. Each lesson marks two or three sounds that are actually in the lines, plus one note on stress or word order. Do one of these this week.
