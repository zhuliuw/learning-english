Speaking

All twenty-four lessons are shadowing. The lines are the same twenty-four listening clips; this page adds no new topic. About fifteen minutes: listen once, open the lines, shadow twice out loud, then record once on your own phone. The recording stays on the phone; this site does not receive it. Each lesson marks two or three sounds that actually appear, plus stress or word order. After the three checks, you can tap four or five words back into order—about half a minute. On the six-month path, do one lesson each week. Lessons 1–4 go in month 1; 5–8 in month 2; 9–12 in month 3; 13–16 in month 4; 17–20 in month 5; 21–24 in month 6.
