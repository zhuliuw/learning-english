Speaking · Lesson 4 · about 15 minutes

What she buys

Skill
Speaking
Time
About 15 minutes
Done when
You have shadowed twice, recorded once on your phone, and asked the three questions

Same market lines, not a new topic. Finish both things and both days. Listen once, speak the lines twice, then record once on your phone. The recording stays on your phone. This site does not receive it. Speaking stops at this lesson.

Material

Listen once before you open the text. You can play it again while you shadow. There is no speed control.

Open the text after you listen

On Sunday, Lin walks to the market. She buys apples and rice. The rice is for Monday. She leaves the apples on the table.

Steps

  1. Listen once. Do not look at the words.
  2. Open the text.
  3. Play it again and speak with it, twice. Say it aloud. Do not only pass over it in your head.
  4. Stop the player. Looking at the text, record once on your own phone. The recording stays there. This page cannot receive it.

Sounds in these lines

The check is still only the three questions. These notes are only sounds that are actually in this clip.

  • rice starts with r. leaves starts with l. Do not swap them.
  • sh in she: do not say see.
  • leaves ends in vz, and apples ends in z. Do not cut them to leave and apple.

Check

Listen to the recording on your phone. Ask only these three questions. Press to mark it yourself. The recording stays on your phone.

One point for a Chinese speaker

Stress Sunday, Lin, market, apples, rice, Monday, and table. Keep and light. apples and rice are two things. Do not say them as one.

Keep one change

From the three questions, write down the one you missed. If you passed all three, write this: the next time you shadow, still ask only these three. There is no next speaking lesson.

Writing all three means you have no priority. Keep one.

These four speaking lessons are the set. Writing is a separate set of four lessons.
