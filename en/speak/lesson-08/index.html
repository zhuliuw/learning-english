Speaking · Lesson 8 · about 15 minutes

The ride after overtime

Skill
Speaking
Time
About 15 minutes
Done when
You have shadowed twice, recorded once on your phone, and used the three checks

The same lines as listening lesson 8. No new topic. Listen once, shadow out loud twice, then record on your own phone. The recording stays on the phone. This site does not receive it.

Material

Listen once without the transcript. Then open it. You may play again while you shadow. There is no speed control.

Open the transcript when you are ready

Hui stays at the office until nine. She books a taxi on her phone. The taxi waits at the gate. She tells the driver her street. She gets home before ten.

Steps

  1. Listen once. Do not look at the words.
  2. Open the transcript.
  3. Play again and shadow twice out loud. Do not only mouth the words.
  4. Stop playback. Looking at the transcript, record once on your phone. The recording stays on the phone. This page cannot receive it.

Sounds in these lines

The three checks stay the same. The notes below mark sounds that actually appear in this lesson.

  • Finish the z at the end of stays. Do not cut it to stay.
  • Stress the first syllable of taxi: TAX-i.
  • Do not drop the second syllable of before: be-FORE.

Self-check

Listen to the recording on your phone. Ask only these three questions. Tap to mark whether you did each one. The recording stays on the phone.

Once more

After the three checks, tap these words into the order of the line. About half a minute.

One point for a Chinese speaker

Stress Hui, stays, office, nine, books, taxi, phone, waits, gate, tells, driver, street, home, ten. Keep at, the, a, on, and her light.

Keep one change

From the three checks, write down the one you missed. If you passed all three, write this: the next time you shadow, still ask only these three. There is no next speaking lesson.

Writing all three means you have no priority. Keep one.

These eight speaking lessons are the set. Writing is separate.
