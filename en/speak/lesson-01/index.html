Speaking · Lesson 1 · about 15 minutes

From open to close

Skill
Speaking
Time
About 15 minutes
Done when
You have shadowed twice, recorded once on your phone, and asked the three questions

Same shop lines, not a new topic. The job is your voice, not another pass at understanding. Listen once, speak the lines twice, then record once on your phone. The recording stays on your phone. This site does not receive it, and there is nowhere to upload it.

Material

Listen once before you open the text. You can play it again while you shadow. There is no speed control.

Open the text after you listen

Chen opens the shop at eight. He puts the bread on the shelf. A customer asks for tea. Chen does not have tea today. He sells the bread and locks the door at six.

Steps

  1. Listen once. Do not look at the words.
  2. Open the text.
  3. Play it again and speak with it, twice. Say it aloud. Do not only pass over it in your head.
  4. Stop the player. Looking at the text, record once on your own phone. The recording stays there. This page cannot receive it.

Sounds in these lines

The check is still only the three questions. These notes are only sounds that are actually in this clip.

  • the, in the bread, the shelf, and the door: touch the upper teeth with the tongue. Do not say ze or de.
  • sh in shelf: round the lips a little. Do not say self.
  • Say the ends of puts and locks. Do not stop at pu or lock.

Check

Listen to the recording on your phone. Ask only these three questions. Press to mark it yourself. The recording stays on your phone.

Once more

After you mark the three checks, tap these words into the order of the line. About half a minute.

One point for a Chinese speaker

Stress names, verbs, times, and things: Chen, opens, bread, eight, six. Keep the and and light. puts the bread on the shelf is still person first: Chen puts the bread.

Keep one change

From the three questions, write down the one you missed. If you passed all three, write this: next lesson, still ask only these three.

Writing all three means you have no priority. Keep one.
