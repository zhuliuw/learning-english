Speaking · Lesson 18 · about 15 minutes

Fixing a bicycle

Skill
Speaking
Time
About 15 minutes
Done when
You have shadowed twice, recorded once on your phone, and used the three checks

Same lines as listening lesson 18. No new topic. Listen once, then say them out loud twice, then record once on your own phone. The recording stays on the phone. This site does not receive it.

Material

Listen once without opening the lines. Open them after. You may play again while you shadow. There is no speed control.

Open after you write

Ning pushes her bike to the stall. The worker finds a flat tire. He patches the tube. The repair takes fifteen minutes. Ning pays thirty yuan in cash.

Steps

  1. Listen once. Do not look at the words.
  2. Open the lines.
  3. Play again and shadow twice out loud. Do not only mouth them.
  4. Stop playback. Looking at the lines, record once on your own phone. The recording stays on the phone. This page cannot receive it.

Sounds in these lines

The three checks stay the same. Below are only sounds that appear in this lesson.

  • Keep the iz ending on pushes clear.
  • Stress the second syllable in fifteen: fif-TEEN.
  • Keep the iz ending on patches clear.

Self-check

Listen to the recording on your phone. Ask only these three. Tap to mark whether you did each. The recording stays on the phone.

One more round

After all three checks are marked, tap these words back into the order of the line. About half a minute.

One note for Chinese speakers

Stress Ning, pushes, bike, stall, worker, finds, flat, tire, patches, tube, repair, takes, fifteen, minutes, pays, thirty, yuan, cash. Keep her, to, the, a, and in light.

Keep one change

From the three checks, pick only one you missed and change that next time. If you did all three, keep this change: next time still ask only these three.

Writing all three means no focus. Keep one.
