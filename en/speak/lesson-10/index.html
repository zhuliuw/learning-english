Speaking · Lesson 10 · about 15 minutes

Lunch in the canteen

Skill
Speaking
Time
About 15 minutes
Done when
You have shadowed twice, recorded once on your phone, and used the three checks

These are the same lines as listening lesson 10. No new topic. Listen once, shadow out loud twice, then record once on your phone. The recording stays on your phone. This site does not receive it.

Material

Listen once without the lines. Open them after. You may play again while you shadow. There is no speed control.

Open after you listen

Bo lines up in the canteen. He pays with his work card. The rice is gone. He takes noodles instead. He sits by the window at noon.

Steps

  1. Listen once. Do not look at the words.
  2. Open the lines.
  3. Play again and shadow twice out loud. Do not only say it in your head.
  4. Stop playback. Looking at the lines, record once on your phone. The recording stays on your phone. This page cannot receive it.

Sounds in these lines

The self-check still uses only those three questions. Below are sounds that actually appear in this lesson.

  • Stress the second syllable of canteen: can-TEEN.
  • Voice the final z in noodles.
  • Keep the n in noon clear.

Self-check

Listen to the recording on your phone. Ask only these three. Tap to mark whether you did each. The recording stays on your phone.

One more round

After all three checks are marked, tap these words back into the order of the line. About half a minute.

One note for Chinese speakers

Stress Bo, lines, canteen, pays, work, card, rice, gone, takes, noodles, sits, window, noon. Keep in, the, with, his, by, at light.

Keep one change

From the three checks, pick only one you missed and change that next time. If you did all three, keep this change: next time still ask only these three.

Writing all three means no focus. Keep one.
