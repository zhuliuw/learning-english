Listening · Lesson 17 · about 15 minutes

Paying utilities online

Skill
Listening
Time
About 15 minutes
Done when
You have said who did what in Chinese, and written one change

Tao pays water and power bills on his phone. Do one thing: in Chinese, write who opened what, which two bills he checked, how much he paid, where the receipt went, and whether he turned off a reminder. Do not take dictation. Write in the box on this page. It stays in this browser for this visit, and it is not uploaded.

Material

Listen first. Do not open the lines. There is no speed control. The clip is under a minute.

Write it here

In Chinese, write who did what. After you write, you can open the lines and the check. It stays in this browser for this visit. It is not uploaded, and it is not scored.

Write first, then open.

One more round

Tap these words back into the order of the line. About half a minute. Finish before you open the lines below.

Open after you write

Tao opens the utility app after dinner. He checks the water and power bills. He pays both in one transfer. The app saves a receipt. Tao turns off the due reminder.

Steps

  1. Listen once. Do not open the lines.
  2. In Chinese, say separately: who opened the app, which two bills he checked, how he paid, where the receipt was saved, and whether the reminder was turned off.
  3. Write first, then open the check. Only then look at the lines.

Check

Open after you write

You can say:

  • Tao opens the utility app after dinner.
  • He checks the water and power bills.
  • He pays both in one transfer.
  • The app saves a receipt.
  • Tao turns off the due reminder.

One note for Chinese speakers

he always points to Tao. The app saves the receipt. Keep who pays and what the app does separate.

Keep one change

After the check, write only one thing to change next time.

  • If you mixed who paid and what saved the receipt, next time keep Tao paying and the app saving. Change only that.
  • If the people and the actions were right, write this: next lesson, still do only one kind of task.

Writing both means no focus. Keep one.
