Listening · Lesson 2 · about 10 minutes

The day on the note

Skill
Listening
Time
About 10 minutes
Done when
You have filled that one word, and written one change

This lesson practices one thing: on the second listen, fill one word. Do not write down the whole clip. Write on paper. This page does not save what you write.

Material

Listen once, just to hear whose situation this is. On the second listen, watch only the blank in this sentence. There is no speed control.

The note says the dinner is on ____.

Open the transcript after you listen

Mina takes the train at seven forty. She reads a short note from her sister. The note says the dinner is on Friday. Mina writes back one word, yes.

Steps

  1. Listen the first time. Do not open the transcript, and do not note every word.
  2. Listen the second time. Fill only the blank above. Write one word.
  3. Then open the check, and only after that the transcript.

Check

Write on paper first, then check

The word is Friday.

The note says the dinner is on Friday.

Keep one change

After you check, write down one thing you will do differently.

  • If the word was wrong, next time use the second listen for that sentence only. Do not restart the whole clip as a dictation. Change only that.
  • If it was right, write this: next lesson, still fill one word, and do not turn it into a full transcript.

Writing both means you have no priority. Keep one.
