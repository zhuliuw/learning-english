Listening · Lesson 5 · about 15 minutes

Clothes from the laundry

Skill
Listening
Time
About 15 minutes
Done when
You have said in Chinese who did what, and written one change

After work, Yan goes to the laundry for her shirts. Again, do one thing: write in Chinese who went where, what she picked up, whether she paid, and how she went home. Do not take dictation. Write in the box on this page. It stays in this browser for this visit, and it is not uploaded. Listening stops at this lesson.

Material

Listen first. Do not open the transcript. There is no speed control. The clip is under a minute.

Write it here

Write in Chinese who did what. After you write, you can open the lines and the check. It stays in this browser for this visit. It is not uploaded, and it is not scored.

Write something first, then open it.

Once more

Tap these words into the order of the line. About half a minute. Then open the lines below.

After you write, open the lines

After work, Yan goes to the laundry. She picks up two shirts. The shirts are for tomorrow. She pays at the counter. She walks home with the bag.

Steps

  1. Listen once. Do not open the transcript.
  2. In Chinese, say who went where, what she picked up, whether she paid, and how she left.
  3. Then open the check, and only after that the transcript.

Check

After you write, open the check

You can say it like this:

  • 下班以后，Yan 去洗衣店。
  • 她取回两件衬衫。
  • 衬衫是明天要用的。
  • 她在柜台付钱。
  • 她提着袋子走回家。

One point for a Chinese speaker

Every she is Yan. Catch the name first, then the later she. shirts and bag are things, not people.

Keep one change

After you check, write down one thing you will do differently.

  • If you split picking up and paying into two people, next time keep only Yan. Change only that.
  • If who and the actions were right, write this: the next time you listen, still do only one kind of task.

Writing both means you have no priority. Keep one. These five listening lessons are the set. The speaking lessons shadow these same clips. Writing is a separate set, and those lessons change a sentence you have read.
