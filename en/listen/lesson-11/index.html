Listening · Lesson 11 · about 15 minutes

Medicine at the pharmacy

Skill
Listening
Time
About 15 minutes
Done when
You have said in Chinese who did what, and written one change

After work, Yan picks up medicine at the pharmacy. Do one thing: write in Chinese who went where, what she showed, what the pharmacist found, who paid, and where the bag went. Do not take dictation. Write in the box on this page. It stays in this browser for this visit, and it is not uploaded.

Material

Listen first. Do not open the lines. There is no speed control. The clip is under one minute.

Write it here

Write in Chinese who did what. After you write, you can open the lines and the check. It stays in this browser for this visit. It is not uploaded, and it is not scored.

Write first, then open.

One more round

Tap these words back into the order of the line. About half a minute. Finish before you open the folded lines below.

Open after you write

After work, Yan walks to the pharmacy. She shows her ID. The pharmacist finds her order. Yan pays at the counter. She puts the bag in her backpack.

Steps

  1. Listen once. Do not open the lines.
  2. In Chinese, say separately: who went to the pharmacy, what she showed, what the pharmacist found, who paid, where the bag went.
  3. After you write, open the check. Only then look at the lines.

Check

Open after you write

You can say:

  • After work, Yan walks to the pharmacy.
  • She shows her ID.
  • The pharmacist finds her order.
  • Yan pays at the counter.
  • She puts the bag in her backpack.

One note for Chinese speakers

she is Yan. The pharmacist is someone else. Do not merge finding the order and paying into one person.

Keep one change

After the check, write only one thing to change next time.

  • If you mixed who found the order and who paid, next time hold that only Yan pays. Change only that.
  • If who and the actions were right, keep this change: next time still do only one job.

Writing both means no focus. Keep one.
