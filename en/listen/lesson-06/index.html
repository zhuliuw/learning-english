Listening · Lesson 6 · about 15 minutes

An hour in the meeting room

Skill
Listening
Time
About 15 minutes
Done when
You have filled that one word, and written one change

Mei books a small meeting room and prints a report. Do one thing: on the second listen, fill only one word. Do not take dictation. Listen first. Do not open the check before you write. Write in the box on this page. It stays in this browser for this visit, and it is not uploaded.

Material

The meeting starts at ____.

Fill it here

Write only that one word. After you write, you can open the lines and the check. It stays in this browser for this visit. It is not uploaded, and it is not scored.

Write first, then open.

Once more

Tap these words into the order of the line. About half a minute. Then open the transcript below.

After you write, open the transcript

Mei books the small meeting room. She prints twelve copies of the report. The printer stops on page eight. Mei waits and finishes the stack. The meeting starts at three.

Steps

  1. Listen once. Do not open the transcript. Do not try to catch every word.
  2. Listen a second time. Fill only the blank in the line above. Write one word.
  3. Then open the check, and only then open the transcript.

Check

After you write, open the check

The word is three.

The meeting starts at three.

One point for a Chinese speaker

The blank sits after at. Clock times often take at: at three, at nine. Do not put the eight from page eight into this blank.

Keep one change

After you check, write down one thing you will do differently.

  • If the word was wrong, next time listen only to that line on the second pass. Do not relearn the whole clip. Change only that.
  • If it was right, write this: the next lesson still fills one word, and does not turn into dictation.

Writing both means you have no priority. Keep one.
