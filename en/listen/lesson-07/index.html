Listening · Lesson 7 · about 15 minutes

A package at the desk

Skill
Listening
Time
About 15 minutes
Done when
You have said in Chinese who did what, and written one change

After lunch, Tao goes to the front desk for a package. Again, do one thing: write in Chinese who went where, what he picked up, whether he signed, and where the package went. Do not take dictation. Write in the box on this page. It stays in this browser for this visit, and it is not uploaded.

Material

Listen first. Do not open the transcript. There is no speed control. The clip is under a minute.

Write it here

Write in Chinese who did what. After you write, you can open the lines and the check. It stays in this browser for this visit. It is not uploaded, and it is not scored.

Write first, then open.

Once more

Tap these words into the order of the line. About half a minute. Then open the transcript below.

After you write, open the transcript

After lunch, Tao goes to the front desk. He picks up a small package. The package is from his sister. He signs the form. He takes the package back to his desk.

Steps

  1. Listen once. Do not open the transcript.
  2. Say in Chinese who went where, what he picked up, whether he signed, and where the package went.
  3. Then open the check. Only then look at the transcript.

Check

After you write, open the check

You can say it like this:

  • After lunch, Tao goes to the front desk.
  • He picks up a small package.
  • The package is from his sister.
  • He signs the form.
  • He takes the package back to his desk.

One point for a Chinese speaker

he always points to Tao. Catch the name first, then the later he. package and form are things, not people.

Keep one change

After you check, write down one thing you will do differently.

  • If you split picking up and signing into two people, next time catch that only Tao acts. Change only that.
  • If who and the actions were right, write this: the next time you listen, still do only one job.

Writing both means you have no priority. Keep one.
