Listening · Lesson 8 · about 15 minutes

The ride after overtime

Skill
Listening
Time
About 15 minutes
Done when
You have filled that one word, and written one change

Hui stays until nine and books a taxi home. Do one thing: on the second listen, fill only one word. Do not take dictation. Listen first. Do not open the check before you write. Write in the box on this page. It stays in this browser for this visit, and it is not uploaded.

Material

She gets home before ____.

Fill it here

Write only that one word. After you write, you can open the lines and the check. It stays in this browser for this visit. It is not uploaded, and it is not scored.

Write first, then open.

Once more

Tap these words into the order of the line. About half a minute. Then open the transcript below.

After you write, open the transcript

Hui stays at the office until nine. She books a taxi on her phone. The taxi waits at the gate. She tells the driver her street. She gets home before ten.

Steps

  1. Listen once. Do not open the transcript. Do not try to catch every word.
  2. Listen a second time. Fill only the blank in the line above. Write one word.
  3. Then open the check, and only then open the transcript.

Check

After you write, open the check

The word is ten.

She gets home before ten.

One point for a Chinese speaker

The blank sits after before. It is the time she gets home. until nine is when she leaves the office. Do not put nine into this blank.

Keep one change

After you check, write down one thing you will do differently.

  • If the word was wrong, next time listen only to that line on the second pass. Do not relearn the whole clip. Change only that.
  • If it was right, write this: the next time you listen, still fill only one word, and do not turn it into dictation.

Writing both means you have no priority. Keep one.
