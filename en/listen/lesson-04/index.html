Listening · Lesson 4 · about 10 minutes

What she buys

Skill
Listening
Time
About 10 minutes
Done when
You have filled that one word, and written one change

This lesson again practices one thing: on the second listen, fill one word. It does not go back to who-did-what, and it is not a full dictation. Write on paper. This page does not save what you write.

Material

The first listen is for the situation. The second listen fills only the sentence below. There is no speed control. Listening stops at this lesson. There is no fifth clip.

She buys apples and ____.

Open the transcript after you listen

On Sunday, Lin walks to the market. She buys apples and rice. The rice is for Monday. She leaves the apples on the table.

Steps

  1. Listen the first time. Do not open the transcript.
  2. Listen the second time. Fill only the blank above. Write one word.
  3. Then open the check, and only after that the transcript.

Check

Write on paper first, then check

The word is rice.

She buys apples and rice.

Keep one change

After you check, write down one thing you will do differently.

  • If the word was wrong, next time repeat only that sentence on the second listen. Change only that.
  • If it was right, write this: the next time you listen, still do only one kind of task.

Writing both means you have no priority. Keep one. These four listening lessons are the set. The speaking lessons shadow these same clips. Writing still has no lesson.
