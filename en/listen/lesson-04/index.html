Listening · Lesson 4 · about 15 minutes

What she buys

Skill
Listening
Time
About 15 minutes
Done when
You have filled that one word, and written one change

On Sunday, Lin walks to the market. Again, do one thing: on the second listen, fill the one blank on the page. Do not go back to who-did-what, and do not take dictation. Do not open the check before you listen. Write in the box on this page. It stays in this browser for this visit, and it is not uploaded. Listening stops at this lesson.

Material

The first listen is for the situation. The second listen fills only the sentence below. There is no speed control. Listening stops at this lesson. There is no fifth clip.

She buys apples and ____.

Fill it here

Write the one missing word. After you write, you can open the lines and the check. It stays in this browser for this visit. It is not uploaded, and it is not scored.

Write something first, then open it.

Once more

Tap these words into the order of the line. About half a minute. Then open the lines below.

After you write, open the lines

On Sunday, Lin walks to the market. She buys apples and rice. The rice is for Monday. She leaves the apples on the table.

Steps

  1. Listen the first time. Do not open the transcript.
  2. Listen the second time. Fill only the blank above. Write one word.
  3. Then open the check, and only after that the transcript.

Check

After you write, open the check

The word is rice.

She buys apples and rice.

One point for a Chinese speaker

The blank is after and, parallel to apples: another thing she buys. Check only that one word. The weekdays in the clip are times, not the word in the blank.

Keep one change

After you check, write down one thing you will do differently.

  • If the word was wrong, next time repeat only that sentence on the second listen. Change only that.
  • If it was right, write this: the next time you listen, still do only one kind of task.

Writing both means you have no priority. Keep one. These four listening lessons are the set. The speaking lessons shadow these same clips. Writing is four other lessons, and they change a sentence you have read.
