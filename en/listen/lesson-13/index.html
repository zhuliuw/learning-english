Listening · Lesson 13 · about 15 minutes

A haircut appointment

Skill
Listening
Time
About 15 minutes
Done when
You have said who did what in Chinese, and written one change

On the weekend, Bo goes for a haircut. Do one thing: in Chinese, write who booked what, what time he arrived, what was cut, how much he paid, and where the receipt went. Do not take dictation. Write in the box on this page. It stays in this browser for this visit, and it is not uploaded.

Material

Listen first. Do not open the lines. There is no speed control. The clip is under a minute.

Write it here

In Chinese, write who did what. After you write, you can open the lines and the check. It stays in this browser for this visit. It is not uploaded, and it is not scored.

Write first, then open.

One more round

After you write, tap these words back into the order of the line. About half a minute. Finish before you open the lines below.

Open after you write

Bo books a haircut for Saturday. He arrives at ten. The stylist cuts his hair short. Bo pays eighty yuan. He puts the receipt in his wallet.

Steps

  1. Listen once. Do not open the lines.
  2. In Chinese, say separately: who booked the cut, what time he arrived, how the stylist cut it, who paid how much, and where the receipt went.
  3. Write first, then open the check. Only then look at the lines.

Check

Open after you write

You can say:

  • Bo books a haircut for Saturday.
  • He arrives at ten.
  • The stylist cuts his hair short.
  • Bo pays eighty yuan.
  • He puts the receipt in his wallet.

One note for Chinese speakers

he always points to Bo. The stylist is a different person. Do not mix cutting and paying into one person.

Keep one change

After the check, write only one thing to change next time.

  • If you mixed cutting and paying into one person, next time catch that only Bo pays. Change only that.
  • If the people and the actions were right, write this: next lesson, still do only one kind of task.

Writing both means no focus. Keep one.
