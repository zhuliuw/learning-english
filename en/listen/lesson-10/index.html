Listening · Lesson 10 · about 15 minutes

Lunch in the canteen

Skill
Listening
Time
About 15 minutes
Done when
You have filled that one word, and written one change

Bo lines up in the canteen and the rice is gone. Do one thing: on the second listen, fill only one word. Do not take dictation. Listen first. Do not open the check before you write. Write in the box on this page. It stays in this browser for this visit, and it is not uploaded.

Material

He sits by the window at ____.

Fill it here

Write only that one word. After you write, you can open the lines and the check. It stays in this browser for this visit. It is not uploaded, and it is not scored.

Write first, then open.

One more round

Tap these words back into the order of the line. About half a minute. Finish before you open the folded lines below.

Open after you write

Bo lines up in the canteen. He pays with his work card. The rice is gone. He takes noodles instead. He sits by the window at noon.

Steps

  1. Listen once. Do not open the lines, and do not write every word.
  2. Listen a second time. Fill only the blank in that one sentence. Write one word.
  3. After you write, open the check, then the lines.

Check

Open after you write

That word is noon.

He sits by the window at noon.

One note for Chinese speakers

The blank comes after at. It is the time he sits down. Do not put noodles in that blank.

Keep one change

After the check, write only one thing to change next time.

  • If that word was wrong, next time on the second listen hear only that sentence. Change only that.
  • If the word was right, keep this change: the next lesson still fills only one word; do not switch to full dictation.

Writing both means no focus. Keep one.
