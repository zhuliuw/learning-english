Listening · Lesson 1 · about 15 minutes

From open to close

Skill
Listening
Time
About 15 minutes
Done when
You have said in Chinese who did what, and written one change

This is Chen’s day at the shop, from opening to locking the door. Do one thing: after you listen, write in Chinese who did what. You do not need every word, and do not translate the clip before you listen. Write in the box on this page. It stays in this browser for this visit, and it is not uploaded. The clip is under a minute. The rest of the fifteen minutes is for writing, the check, and the note on word order.

Material

Listen first. Do not open the transcript. There is no speed control. The clip is under a minute.

Write it here

In Chinese, write who did what. After you write, you can open the lines and the check. It stays in this browser for this visit. It is not uploaded, and it is not scored.

Write something first, then open it.

Once more

Tap these words into the order of the line. About half a minute. Then open the lines below.

After you write, open the lines

Chen opens the shop at eight. He puts the bread on the shelf. A customer asks for tea. Chen does not have tea today. He sells the bread and locks the door at six.

Steps

  1. Listen once. Do not pause to look words up, and do not open the transcript.
  2. Say in Chinese who did what. People and actions are enough. If a place is wrong, write it down anyway.
  3. Then open the check. Look at the transcript only after that.

Check

After you write, open the check

You can say it like this:

  • 陈八点打开店。
  • 他把面包放到架子上。
  • 一位顾客要茶。
  • 陈今天没有茶。
  • 他卖掉面包，六点锁门。

One point for a Chinese speaker

The line is person, then verb, then thing, then place. He puts the bread on the shelf is not “the bread puts.” does not have means he does not have it. Do not say no have. When you check, match the person to the action.

Keep one change

After you check, write down one thing you will do differently.

  • If two people’s actions got mixed, next time separate who. Change only that.
  • If the people and the actions were right, write this: next lesson, still say who did what, and do not switch to catching every word.

Writing both means you have no priority. Keep one.
