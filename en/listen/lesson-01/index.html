Listening · Lesson 1 · about 10 minutes

From open to close

Skill
Listening
Time
About 10 minutes
Done when
You have said in Chinese who did what, and written one change

This lesson practices one thing: after you listen, say in Chinese who did what. You do not need every word. Write on paper. This page does not save what you write.

Material

Listen first. Do not open the transcript. There is no speed control. The clip is under a minute.

Open the transcript after you listen

Chen opens the shop at eight. He puts the bread on the shelf. A customer asks for tea. Chen does not have tea today. He sells the bread and locks the door at six.

Steps

  1. Listen once. Do not pause to look words up, and do not open the transcript.
  2. Say in Chinese who did what. People and actions are enough. If a place is wrong, write it down anyway.
  3. Then open the check. Look at the transcript only after that.

Check

Write on paper first, then check

You can say it like this:

  • 陈八点打开店。
  • 他把面包放到架子上。
  • 一位顾客要茶。
  • 陈今天没有茶。
  • 他卖掉面包，六点锁门。

Keep one change

After you check, write down one thing you will do differently.

  • If two people’s actions got mixed, next time separate who. Change only that.
  • If the people and the actions were right, write this: next lesson, still say who did what, and do not switch to catching every word.

Writing both means you have no priority. Keep one.
