Listening · Lesson 9 · about 15 minutes

Calling building maintenance

Skill
Listening
Time
About 15 minutes
Done when
You have said in Chinese who did what, and written one change

Lin finds a leak and calls the building desk. Do one thing: write in Chinese who found what, who called, when the worker came, what was fixed, and who signed. Do not take dictation. Write in the box on this page. It stays in this browser for this visit, and it is not uploaded.

Material

Listen first. Do not open the lines. There is no speed control. The clip is under one minute.

Write it here

Write in Chinese who did what. After you write, you can open the lines and the check. It stays in this browser for this visit. It is not uploaded, and it is not scored.

Write first, then open.

One more round

After you write, tap these words back into the order of the line. About half a minute. Finish before you open the folded lines below.

Open after you write

Lin finds a leak under the sink. She calls the building desk. A worker comes at four. He fixes the pipe. Lin signs the work form.

Steps

  1. Listen once. Do not open the lines.
  2. In Chinese, say separately: who found the leak, who called, when the worker came, what was fixed, who signed.
  3. After you write, open the check. Only then look at the lines.

Check

Open after you write

You can say:

  • Lin finds a leak under the sink.
  • She calls the building desk.
  • A worker comes at four.
  • He fixes the pipe.
  • Lin signs the work form.

One note for Chinese speakers

she is Lin. Hold the name first, then track she. he is the worker, not Lin.

Keep one change

After the check, write only one thing to change next time.

  • If you mixed up who fixed and who signed, next time separate the worker and Lin. Change only that.
  • If who and the actions were right, keep this change: next time still do only one job.

Writing both means no focus. Keep one.
