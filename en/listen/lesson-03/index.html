Listening · Lesson 3 · about 10 minutes

Who calls, who sends

Skill
Listening
Time
About 10 minutes
Done when
You have said in Chinese who did what, and written one change

This lesson still practices one thing: after you listen, say in Chinese who did what. The task type does not change. Write on paper. This page does not save what you write.

Material

Listen first. Do not open the transcript. There is no speed control. The clip is under a minute.

Open the transcript after you listen

The manager calls at noon. She asks for the report. David says he will send it after lunch. He eats, then he sends the file. The manager does not call again.

Steps

  1. Listen once. Do not open the transcript.
  2. In Chinese, separate who calls, who asks for what, and who does something after that.
  3. Then open the check. Look at the transcript only after that.

Check

Write on paper first, then check

You can say it like this:

  • 经理中午打电话来。
  • 她要那份报告。
  • 戴维说他午饭后会发出去。
  • 他吃完，然后把文件发出去。
  • 经理没有再打来。

Keep one change

After you check, write down one thing you will do differently.

  • If the manager’s action and David’s action swapped, next time catch who is speaking. Change only that.
  • If the people and the actions were right, write this: when the next lesson changes the task, still do not transcribe the whole clip.

Writing both means you have no priority. Keep one.
