Listening · Lesson 3 · about 15 minutes

Who calls, who sends

Skill
Listening
Time
About 15 minutes
Done when
You have said in Chinese who did what, and written one change

There is a phone call at noon. The manager is one person. David is another. Still do one thing: write in Chinese who calls, who asks for what, and who does what later. Do not merge the two people. Write in the box on this page. It stays in this browser for this visit, and it is not uploaded.

Material

Listen first. Do not open the transcript. There is no speed control. The clip is under a minute.

Write it here

In Chinese, write who did what. After you write, you can open the lines and the check. It stays in this browser for this visit. It is not uploaded, and it is not scored.

Write something first, then open it.

After you write, open the lines

The manager calls at noon. She asks for the report. David says he will send it after lunch. He eats, then he sends the file. The manager does not call again.

Steps

  1. Listen once. Do not open the transcript.
  2. In Chinese, separate who calls, who asks for what, and who does something after that.
  3. Then open the check. Look at the transcript only after that.

Check

After you write, open the check

You can say it like this:

  • 经理中午打电话来。
  • 她要那份报告。
  • 戴维说他午饭后会发出去。
  • 他吃完，然后把文件发出去。
  • 经理没有再打来。

One point for a Chinese speaker

she and he change person. Anchor on the last name you heard, then see whether the next she or he is still that person. Do not merge the manager and David.

Keep one change

After you check, write down one thing you will do differently.

  • If the manager’s action and David’s action swapped, next time catch who is speaking. Change only that.
  • If the people and the actions were right, write this: when the next lesson changes the task, still do not transcribe the whole clip.

Writing both means you have no priority. Keep one.
