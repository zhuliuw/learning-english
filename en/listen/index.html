Listening · twenty-four lessons

Listening

Twenty-four clips under a minute, spoken the same clear way: opening a shop, a note, a call, the market, laundry, a meeting room, a package, a taxi after overtime, building maintenance, canteen lunch, the pharmacy, parking, a haircut, filling up, a new SIM, the gym, utility bills, bike repair, a health check, a passport, moving day, hotel check-in, the airport, and year-end invoices. About fifteen minutes a lesson. The audio is short; time goes to writing and one note. Do one job: in Chinese, say who did what, or on the second listen fill only one word. After you write, you can tap four or five words from the line back into order—about half a minute. The transcript stays folded. On the six-month path, do one lesson each week. Lessons 1–4 go in month 1; 5–8 in month 2; 9–12 in month 3; 13–16 in month 4; 17–20 in month 5; 21–24 in month 6. Catching the idea is enough. There is no speed control on the page.
