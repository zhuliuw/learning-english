Listening · four lessons

Listening

For adults who use Chinese. Four clips, each under a minute, in the same clear reading: a shop, a note, a phone call, a market. About fifteen minutes a lesson. The clip is short. The time goes to writing and one note. Do one thing: say in Chinese who did what, or fill one word on the second listen. The transcript stays closed. Do one of these this week. You do not have to hear every word, and there is no speed control.

How this step works

  • Lesson 1 · say who did what · about 15 minutes From open to close
  • Lesson 2 · one word · about 15 minutes The day on the note
  • Lesson 3 · say who did what · about 15 minutes Who calls, who sends
  • Lesson 4 · one word · about 15 minutes What she buys

Speaking uses these four clips, on another day. Reading and writing are not added here.
