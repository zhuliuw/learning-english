Listening

Four clips, each under a minute, all in the same clear reading. Each lesson asks for one thing: say in Chinese who did what, or fill one word on the second listen. The transcript stays closed. You do not have to hear every word, and there is no speed control.

  • Lesson 1 · say who did what · about 10 minutes From open to close
  • Lesson 2 · one word · about 10 minutes The day on the note
  • Lesson 3 · say who did what · about 10 minutes Who calls, who sends
  • Lesson 4 · one word · about 10 minutes What she buys
