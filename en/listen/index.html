There is no listening lesson yet.

What will be added

Each lesson in the listening phase is planned to have only this:

  • one very short audio file, hosted with this site
  • the transcript of that same clip, kept closed until you have listened
  • one task after you listen, such as saying in Chinese who did what, or filling one word you missed

Not a listening exam, and not a demand that you hear every word.

What you can do now

Do reading lesson 1. The move in that lesson, who does what, is the same move listening will use.
