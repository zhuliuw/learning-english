Listening · Lesson 22 · about 15 minutes

Hotel check-in

Skill
Listening
Time
About 15 minutes
Done when
You have filled that one word, and written one change

Lan checks into a hotel on a work trip. Do one thing: on the second listen, fill only one word. Do not take dictation. Listen first. Do not open the check before you write. Write in the box on this page. It stays in this browser for this visit, and it is not uploaded.

Material

Her room is on the ____ floor.

Fill it here

Write only that one word. After you write, you can open the lines and the check. It stays in this browser for this visit. It is not uploaded, and it is not scored.

Write first, then open.

One more round

After you write, tap these words back into the order of the line. About half a minute. Finish before you open the lines below.

Open after you write

Lan shows her booking on the phone. The clerk asks for her ID. Her room is on the twelfth floor. She takes the key card. The elevator stops twice on the way up.

Steps

  1. Listen once. Do not open the lines, and do not try to catch every word.
  2. Listen again. Fill only the blank in the line above. Write one word.
  3. Write first, then open the check, then look at the lines.

Check

Open after you write

The word is twelfth.

Her room is on the twelfth floor.

One note for Chinese speakers

The blank sits after the. It is the floor number as an ordinal. twice is how often the elevator stops. Do not put twice in this blank.

Keep one change

After the check, write only one thing to change next time.

  • If the word was wrong, next time listen only to that one line on the second pass. Change only that.
  • If it was right, write this: next lesson, still fill one word, and do not turn it into a full transcript.

Writing both means no focus. Keep one.
