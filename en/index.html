For adults

Four lessons a week. One job each.

For adults who use Chinese and need English at work, and who are not fluent yet. Not a children’s start, and not an exam paper. In a week, do one listening, one reading, one speaking, and one writing lesson. About fifteen minutes each: see the one job, do it, and keep one change. Ticks stay on paper.

See this week Read “How to learn”

How to start

  1. Read How to learn first. About ten minutes. You should be able to say three things: one job per lesson; one change when you finish; a missed day does not need to be made up.
  2. Follow this week. On five days, do one thing each: listen, read, speak, write, then a day that only looks at that one change, or a rest. The other two days have no lesson.
  3. When the week is done, change lessons. Do not add another lesson that day. Next week is still once each, chosen from the lesson index.

Listening, speaking, reading, writing

  • Listening Four lessons Four clips under a minute: a shop, a note, a phone call, a market. Say in Chinese who did what, or fill one word. Do one this week.
  • Speaking Four lessons Shadow a clip you already heard. Say it aloud twice, then record once on your phone. The recording stays there. Do one this week.
  • Reading Five lessons The first two find who does what. The next three ask what he, she, they, and it point to. Commute, work messages, weekend. Do one this week.
  • Writing Four lessons Put your own day, time, or place into a sentence you have read. Write on paper. Four checks, no score. Do one this week.

What this site does not do

  • No account and no streak. Ticks stay on the page you print.
  • No ads, and no visit data collected.
  • Not exam practice, and no promise that a few weeks will make you fluent.
