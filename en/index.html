Phase 1

Few lessons, so the method stays clear.

This site is for Chinese speakers. If you have studied English and still get stuck when you have to listen, speak, read, or write, start by making the method small. The two starting pieces stay the same: read the method, then do reading lesson 1. Listening now also has four short clips.

Read “How to learn” Do lesson 1

How to start

  1. Read “How to learn.” About ten minutes. You should be able to say: one lesson, one job, and one change to keep.
  2. Do reading lesson 1. About fifteen minutes. It is short on purpose. Then stop.
  3. Do not look for speaking or writing lessons yet. Those pages still have no practice. The listening clips can wait until after reading lesson 1.

Listening, speaking, reading, writing

  • Listening Four lessons Each clip is under a minute. Say in Chinese who did what, or fill one word on the second listen.
  • Speaking No lesson yet The speaking phase uses shadowing and a check on your own phone. Recordings stay there.
  • Reading Five lessons The first two find who does what. The next three ask what a pronoun points to.
  • Writing No lesson yet The writing phase asks you to rewrite a sentence you have read, with a short model.

Do not expect these yet

  • No account, and no record of your progress.
  • No ads, and no visit data collected.
  • No promise that this phase will make you fluent.
