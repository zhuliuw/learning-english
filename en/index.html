This week

Do this week first.

This site is for Chinese speakers. Listening, reading, speaking, and writing all have lessons. This week, do one of each, then rest or look only at the one change you wrote down. Ticks stay on paper.

See this week Read “How to learn”

How to start

  1. If you have not read “How to learn,” read that page first. About ten minutes. You should be able to say: one lesson, one job, and one change to keep.
  2. Follow this week. Listen, read, speak, and write once each, then rest or look only at that one change.
  3. Then stop. Next week, pick other lessons from the lesson index. Still once each.

Listening, speaking, reading, writing

  • Listening Four lessons Each clip is under a minute. Say in Chinese who did what, or fill one word on the second listen.
  • Speaking Four lessons Shadow the four clips you already heard. Record on your phone. This site does not receive it.
  • Reading Five lessons The first two find who does what. The next three ask what a pronoun points to.
  • Writing Four lessons Swap your own detail into a sentence you have read. Check it yourself. This site does not score it.

Do not expect these yet

  • No account. Ticks stay on the printed list. This site does not store them.
  • No ads, and no visit data collected.
  • No promise that this phase will make you fluent.
