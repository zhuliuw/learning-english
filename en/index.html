For adults

Four lessons a week. One job each.

You already work. You use English when you need it, and speaking and writing still take practice. This site is for that stage. In a week, do one listening, one reading, one speaking, and one writing lesson. Listening, speaking, and writing take about fifteen minutes. Reading takes about twenty. Open a lesson, see the one job, do it, and keep one change for next time. Ticks stay on paper.

The order this week

  1. 1 · ListenHear a short clip, then say in Chinese who did what.
  2. 2 · ReadRead one passage. Mark who and the verb, or what a pronoun points to.
  3. 3 · SpeakSay a clip you have heard. Aloud, then record it on your phone.
  4. 4 · WriteChange one detail in a sentence you have read.

See this week Read “How to learn”

How to start

  1. Read How to learn first. About ten minutes. You should be able to say three things: one job per lesson; one change when you finish; a missed day does not need to be made up.
  2. Follow this week. On five days, do one thing each: listen, read, speak, write, then a day that only looks at that one change, or a rest. The other two days have no lesson.
  3. When the week is done, change lessons. Do not add another lesson that day. Next week is still once each, chosen from the lesson index.

Listening, speaking, reading, writing

  • Listening Five lessons Five clips, each under a minute: opening a shop, a note, a phone call, a market, clothes from the laundry. After you listen, say in Chinese who did what, or fill one word. Then you can put four or five words from that line back in order. About half a minute. Do one this week.
  • Speaking Five lessons Shadow a clip you already heard. Say it aloud twice, then record once on your own phone. The recording stays there. Do one this week.
  • Reading Six lessons The first two find who does what. The next four ask what he, she, they, it, and them point to. A commute, work messages, a weekend, dinner: the kind of day you already have. Do one this week.
  • Writing Five lessons Put your own day, time, or place into a sentence you have read. Write in the box on the lesson page. Four checks, no score. Do one this week.

What this site does not do

  • No account, and no streak. Ticks stay on the page you print.
  • No ads. What you type in a lesson stays in this browser for this visit.
  • These are everyday lessons. Fluency takes longer than a few weeks of them.
