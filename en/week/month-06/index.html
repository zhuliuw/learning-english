Month 6

Four weeks. Each week, do one listening, one reading, one speaking, and one writing lesson, then rest or look at one change. Listening, speaking, and writing take about fifteen minutes. Reading takes about twenty. Speaking shadows the same audio as that week’s listening. Ticks stay on paper. This site does not remember what you checked.

Back to six months for the overview. Finish month 5 first if you can. Month 6 uses lessons 21–24.

Four weeks

  1. Week 1Listen “Loading the moving van”, read “When parents visit”, speak “Loading the moving van”, write “The evening you clear the spare room”, then rest or review one change.
  2. Week 2Listen “Hotel check-in”, read “A coworker’s wedding”, speak “Hotel check-in”, write “The afternoon you leave for a wedding”, then rest or review one change.
  3. Week 3Listen “Airport check-in”, read “Asking for annual leave”, speak “Airport check-in”, write “The morning you fill the leave form”, then rest or review one change.
  4. Week 4Listen “Year-end invoices”, read “The year-end review meeting”, speak “Year-end invoices”, write “The day you join the year-end review”, then rest or review one change.
