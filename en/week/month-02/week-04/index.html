Week 4

This is week 4 of month 2. Do five things across five days; leave two days free. Listening, speaking, and writing take about fifteen minutes each. Reading takes about twenty. On the fifth day, rest or look only at the one change you wrote. Tick the boxes on paper. This site does not remember what you checked.

Back to month 2 for the four-week overview. If you have not read How to learn, read that page first.

Checklist

  1. Listen once Do The ride after overtime. To swap, pick another listening lesson from the lesson index.
  2. Read once Do The notice in the hallway. To swap, pick another reading lesson from the index.
  3. Speak once Shadow The ride after overtime. The audio matches this week’s listening clip. Record on your phone. This site does not receive it.
  4. Write once Do The day your water stops. Write in the box on the lesson page. There is no score.
  5. Rest, or review one change Do not open a new lesson. If one spot from this week is weak, look only at that.
