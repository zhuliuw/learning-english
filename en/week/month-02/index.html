Month 2

Four weeks. Each week, do one listening, one reading, one speaking, and one writing lesson, then rest or look at one change. Listening, speaking, and writing take about fifteen minutes. Reading takes about twenty. Speaking shadows the same audio as that week’s listening. Ticks stay on paper. This site does not remember what you checked.

Back to three months for the overview. Finish month 1 first if you can. After month 2, go to month 3. Month 2 uses lessons 5–8; reading lessons 5 and 6 fill the first two weeks, and lessons 7 and 8 fill the last two.

Four weeks

  1. Week 1Listen “Clothes from the laundry”, read “The delayed train”, speak “Clothes from the laundry”, write “The time you leave the office”, then rest or review one change.
  2. Week 2Listen “An hour in the meeting room”, read “The kitchen before dinner”, speak “An hour in the meeting room”, write “The time you get home”, then rest or review one change.
  3. Week 3Listen “A package at the desk”, read “An hour at the clinic”, speak “A package at the desk”, write “The time you leave for the clinic”, then rest or review one change.
  4. Week 4Listen “The ride after overtime”, read “The notice in the hallway”, speak “The ride after overtime”, write “The day your water stops”, then rest or review one change.
