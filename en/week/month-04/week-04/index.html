Week 4

This is week 4 of Month 4. Do five things across five days; the other two days have no lesson. Listening, speaking, and writing take about fifteen minutes each. Reading takes about twenty. On the fifth day, rest, or only look at the one change you wrote. Tick the boxes on paper. This site does not remember what you checked.

Back to Month 4 for the four-week overview. If you have not read How to learn, read that page first.

Checklist

  1. One listening lesson Do Joining the gym. To swap, pick another listening lesson from Lessons.
  2. One reading lesson Do The weekend wet market. To swap, pick another reading lesson from the index.
  3. One speaking lesson Shadow Joining the gym. Same audio as this week’s listening. Record on your own phone; this site does not receive it.
  4. One writing lesson Do The morning you go to the market. Write in the box on the lesson page. No score.
  5. Rest, or review one change Do not open a new lesson. If one thing went wrong this week, look only at that.
