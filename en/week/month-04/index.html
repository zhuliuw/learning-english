Month 4

Four weeks. Each week, do one listening, one reading, one speaking, and one writing lesson, then rest or look at one change. Listening, speaking, and writing take about fifteen minutes. Reading takes about twenty. Speaking shadows the same audio as that week’s listening. Ticks stay on paper. This site does not remember what you checked.

Back to six months for the overview. Finish month 3 first if you can. Month 4 uses lessons 13–16.

Four weeks

  1. Week 1Listen “A haircut appointment”, read “Signing in at the community clinic”, speak “A haircut appointment”, write “The morning you go to the clinic”, then rest or review one change.
  2. Week 2Listen “Filling up at the station”, read “The morning subway rush”, speak “Filling up at the station”, write “The time you board in the rush”, then rest or review one change.
  3. Week 3Listen “A new SIM at the shop”, read “A fire drill at the office”, speak “A new SIM at the shop”, write “The time your drill starts”, then rest or review one change.
  4. Week 4Listen “Joining the gym”, read “The weekend wet market”, speak “Joining the gym”, write “The morning you go to the market”, then rest or review one change.
