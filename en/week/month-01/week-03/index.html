Week 3

This is week 3 of the month path. Five things on any five days. The other two days have no lesson. Listen, speak, and write about fifteen minutes each; reading about twenty. On the fifth day, rest, or look only at the one change you wrote down. Tick the boxes on paper. This site does not remember what you checked.

Back to month 1 for the four-week overview. If you have not read How to learn, read that page first.

List

  1. One listening lesson Do Who calls, who sends. To swap, pick another listening lesson from the lesson index.
  2. One reading lesson Do Three work messages. To swap, pick another reading lesson from the index.
  3. One speaking lesson Shadow Who calls, who sends. The audio matches this week’s listening clip. Record on your phone. This site does not receive it.
  4. One writing lesson Do The day the meeting moves. Write in the box on the lesson page. There is no score.
  5. Rest, or review one change Do not open a new lesson. If one thing from this week did not hold, look only at that.
