Month 5

Four weeks. Each week, do one listening, one reading, one speaking, and one writing lesson, then rest or look at one change. Listening, speaking, and writing take about fifteen minutes. Reading takes about twenty. Speaking shadows the same audio as that week’s listening. Ticks stay on paper. This site does not remember what you checked.

Back to six months for the overview. Finish month 4 first if you can. Month 5 uses lessons 17–20.

Four weeks

  1. Week 1Listen “Paying utilities online”, read “Leaving a laptop for repair”, speak “Paying utilities online”, write “The time you leave a machine for repair”, then rest or review one change.
  2. Week 2Listen “Fixing a bicycle”, read “Signing up for the team outing”, speak “Fixing a bicycle”, write “The day you sign up for the outing”, then rest or review one change.
  3. Week 3Listen “A company health check”, read “Renewing the lease”, speak “A company health check”, write “The evening you renew the lease”, then rest or review one change.
  4. Week 4Listen “Renewing a passport”, read “The yearly car inspection”, speak “Renewing a passport”, write “The time you go for inspection”, then rest or review one change.
