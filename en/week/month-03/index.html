Month 3

Four weeks. Each week, do one listening, one reading, one speaking, and one writing lesson, then rest or look at one change. Listening, speaking, and writing take about fifteen minutes. Reading takes about twenty. Speaking shadows the same audio as that week’s listening. Ticks stay on paper. This site does not remember what you checked.

Back to three months for the overview. Finish month 1 and month 2 first if you can. Month 3 uses lessons 9–12.

Four weeks

  1. Week 1Listen “Calling building maintenance”, read “An hour at the bank”, speak “Calling building maintenance”, write “The time you leave for the bank”, then rest or review one change.
  2. Week 2Listen “Lunch in the canteen”, read “After the late shift at the store”, speak “Lunch in the canteen”, write “The time your late shift ends”, then rest or review one change.
  3. Week 3Listen “Medicine at the pharmacy”, read “Returning books at the library”, speak “Medicine at the pharmacy”, write “The day you return the books”, then rest or review one change.
  4. Week 4Listen “Paying for parking”, read “A delivery downstairs”, speak “Paying for parking”, write “The evening you pick up a delivery”, then rest or review one change.
