Five things · printable

This week

For adults who already work. Five things on any five days. The other two days have no lesson. Listen, speak, and write once each, about fifteen minutes, and read once, about twenty. On the fifth day, rest, or look only at the one change you wrote down. Tick the boxes on paper. This site does not remember what you checked.

If you have not read How to learn, read that page first. One lesson, one job, and one change to keep.

List

  1. One listening lesson This week, try Clothes from the laundry. If you have done it, or want an earlier lesson, pick another listening lesson from the lesson index.
  2. One reading lesson This week, try reading lesson 6: The kitchen before dinner. If you have done it, or want an earlier lesson, pick another reading lesson from the index.
  3. One speaking lesson This week, shadow Clothes from the laundry. Record on your phone. This site does not receive it. If you have done it, or want an earlier lesson, pick another speaking lesson from the index.
  4. One writing lesson This week, try The time you leave the office. Write in the box on the lesson page. There is no score. If you have done it, or want an earlier lesson, pick another writing lesson from the index.
  5. Rest, or review one change Do not open a new lesson. If one thing from this week did not hold, look only at that.

Use the browser’s print to keep this page, and tick it on paper. Next week is the same five things. Pick the lessons from the lesson index.
