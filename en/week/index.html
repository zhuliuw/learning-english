Four weeks · printable

This month

For adults who already work. One month is four weeks. Each week, do one listening, one reading, one speaking, and one writing lesson, then rest or look at one change. Listening, speaking, and writing take about fifteen minutes. Reading takes about twenty. Speaking shadows the same audio as that week’s listening. Ticks stay on paper. This site does not remember what you checked.

If you have not read How to learn, read that page first. One lesson, one job, and one change to keep. A missed day does not need to be made up. After the four weeks, lessons 5 and 6 stay in the lesson index if you want another week on your own.

Four weeks

  1. Week 1Listen “From open to close”, read “Find who does what”, speak “From open to close”, write “Your own deadline”, then rest or review one change.
  2. Week 2Listen “The day on the note”, read “The early bus”, speak “The day on the note”, write “The time you leave”, then rest or review one change.
  3. Week 3Listen “Who calls, who sends”, read “Three work messages”, speak “Who calls, who sends”, write “The day the meeting moves”, then rest or review one change.
  4. Week 4Listen “What she buys”, read “Saturday’s plan”, speak “What she buys”, write “Where and when you meet”, then rest or review one change.

How to use a week

Open one week, print that page, and tick it on paper. Do not put two weeks into the same evening. To swap a lesson, use the links on that week’s page, or pick another from the index.
