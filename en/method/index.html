Method

How to learn

For someone who already works. This page is how the week actually goes: what you do, what you leave, and how long a lesson takes. About ten minutes. The 中文 link is this same page in Chinese.

How long, and what you do

Plan fifteen minutes for a lesson, and stop by twenty-five. A reading lesson takes about twenty minutes, because the passage is longer than a few lines. Listening, speaking, and writing still take about fifteen. A listening clip is under a minute. The rest of the time is for writing and the check. One move only: say who did what, mark what a pronoun points to, or put your own day into one sentence. When that move is done, stop for the day. If you do not finish, leave the second lesson for another evening.

Read the Chinese task before the English

Open a lesson and read the paragraph under the title. See what “done” means. Then play the clip or read the English. On the first pass, leave the dictionary closed. On a reading lesson, look up one word at most.

Keep the input short, then use it

Listening: after the first pass, write in Chinese who did what, or fill one blank on the second pass. Reading: mark who and the verb, or write what the pronoun points to, then answer three questions. Speaking: listen once, open the lines, say them twice, and record once on your phone. Writing: change only the given sentence, in the box on the lesson page. After a listening lesson, you can also put four or five words from that line back in order. About half a minute. If you only look and never write the one thing, the lesson is still unfinished.

Build the sentence in Chinese first

Before you write the English, put “who, did what, when” in the margin. Then write that sentence. The Chinese frame can come first.

After you check, keep one change

The check is there so you know what to change next. Write one thing you will do differently. Three notes, and next time you will not know which one to pick up.

A missed day

There is no streak. Open “this week” another day and do the next thing you have not done. The missed days can stay missed. Two lessons do not need to share one evening.

What to skip

  • Do not upload a recording. A speaking recording stays on your phone. This site cannot receive it.
  • Do not hand work in. Nobody marks your sentence, and there is no score.
  • What you type on a lesson stays in this browser for this visit. It is not uploaded, and there is no account.
  • Do not memorize a word list, do not translate a whole passage, and do not do multiple-choice questions.
  • Do not practice listening and speaking in the same lesson. Speaking uses a clip you have heard, on another day.

If you can work this way, set up this week. Listen, read, speak, and write once each, then use one day only for that change, or rest.
