Method

How to learn

These rules are ordinary, and they do not make you fluent. They are here so that at the end of a lesson you can say what you practiced and what you will change next time. About ten minutes. The 中文 link is the same page in Chinese, not a different course.

One job per lesson

Keep a lesson to fifteen or twenty-five minutes. Practice one move, such as finding who does what. Stop when you finish. If you do not finish, do not add two more lessons that night.

See the task in Chinese first

Read the goal in Chinese and check what “done” means before you look at the English. Do not open the passage and start looking up words.

Keep the input short, then use it

A short listen or a short read is enough. Then do one small thing: answer a question, say who did what, or change a sentence. If you only take the text in, the lesson is not done.

Build the frame in Chinese

Write “who, did what, when” in Chinese, then put it into English. Do not treat “think only in English” as the starting point.

After you check, keep one change

Answers are for editing, not for a score. Write down one thing you will do differently. Three notes means no priority.

A missed day is fine

There is no streak. Come back another day and do the next thing you have not done. You do not owe the missed days.

What this phase cannot do

There is nowhere to upload a recording, and nobody to mark your sentence. Listening, speaking, and writing have short lessons. A speaking recording stays on your phone. Writing stays on paper. You check the list yourself. There is no score.

If you can work this way, do lesson 1. About fifteen minutes.
