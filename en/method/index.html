Method

How to learn

For adults who already work. This page says what to do this week, what to skip, and how long a lesson takes. About ten minutes. The 中文 link is the same page in Chinese, not a different course.

How long, and what you do

Keep a lesson to fifteen minutes, and stop by twenty-five. A listening clip is under a minute. The rest of the time is for writing and the check. One move only: say who did what, mark what a pronoun points to, or put your own day into one sentence. When that move is done, do not add a second lesson the same day. If you do not finish, do not add two lessons that night.

Read the Chinese task before the English

Open a lesson and read the paragraph under the title. Check what “done” means. Then play the clip or read the English. Do not look up words on the first pass. On a reading lesson, look up one word at most.

Keep the input short, then use it

Listening: after the first pass, write in Chinese who did what, or fill one blank on the second pass. Reading: mark who and the verb, or write what the pronoun points to, then answer three questions. Speaking: listen once, open the lines, say them twice, and record once on your phone. Writing: change only the given sentence, on paper. If you only look at the material, the lesson is not done.

Build the sentence in Chinese first

Before you write English, put “who, did what, when” in the margin, then put that into English. Do not start from “think only in English.”

After you check, keep one change

The check is for the next change, not for a score. Write one thing you will do differently. Three notes means no priority.

A missed day

There is no streak. Open “this week” another day and do the next thing you have not done. You do not owe the missed days, and you do not put two lessons into one evening.

What to skip

  • Do not upload a recording. A speaking recording stays on your phone. This site cannot receive it.
  • Do not hand work in. Nobody marks your sentence, and there is no score.
  • Do not memorize a word list, do not translate a whole passage, and do not do multiple-choice questions.
  • Do not practice listening and speaking in the same lesson. Speaking uses a clip you have heard, on another day.

If you can work this way, set up this week. Listen, read, speak, and write once each, then use one day only for that change, or rest.
